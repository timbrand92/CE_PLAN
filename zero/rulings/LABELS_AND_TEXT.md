# Current rulings: Labels and text (labels, tags, cover text, what prints, APPROX., TBC)

Current rulings as of 20 Nov 2026 (Melbourne), from decisions D-001 to D-383 (D-298 to D-383 filed 20 Nov 2026). If this page and DECISIONS.md disagree, DECISIONS.md wins and this page is stale.

## What is decided now

### Where labels sit and how they move

1. **Text and parts sit at a set distance from the object,** a setting that is normally always the same, moved only to clear a clash (D-012).
2. **Each part sits in one standard spot on every pipe kind;** the invert level slides up and down the pipe only to clear a clash (D-041). **Made specific by D-362 and D-365 (20 Nov):** an invert may also flip to the label's side when clashing; flip first, slide second (item 28).
3. **Grips let the text, invert text and arrow move perpendicular or parallel to the line; cover level text and the pit label can be moved and flipped;** all scenarios covered (D-037).
4. **A label moved by hand stays where it was put when the pipe changes;** the wording updates (D-014).
5. **When a pit is moved, its tag and cover level text go with it and a hand-moved label keeps its offset in both directions** (D-181).
6. **A flip must be discoverable,** never only on a right-click (D-049 item 5).
7. **The tank note has a leader arrow back to the tank** (D-039).

### What prints

8. **The pipe label prints the design grade as typed, for example "1 IN 75";** inverts are rounded to two decimals (D-154, replacing D-123 for the label). **Extended by D-238 (18 Nov):** the label ends "MIN." when both inverts are not given (item 17).
9. **A typed-over label keeps the wording, is flagged as no longer matching, and one click lets it follow again** (D-182).
10. **Retyping a pit tag renames the pit;** levels and grades are never changed by editing their text (D-185).
11. **An assumed figure prints "APPROX." in front,** for example "APPROX. IL 4.95", with one standing note on the sheet; APPROX. goes when confirmed (D-177).
12. **A figure not known yet prints "TBC"** (D-178).
13. **Twin detention pipes have one label** saying "2 x 225" (D-082).
14. **The criteria box prints the baffle height under "HEAD"** (D-023).
15. **Text sizes are standardised in one place** (D-013), and model text and label sizes suit a 1:100 viewport (D-194).
16. **Previews show the arrow and text as they will look on screen** (D-162).

### Added 18 Nov 2026, D-230 to D-297

17. **"MIN." on a pipe label (D-236, D-238):** "MIN. whenever both inverts are NOT given". With one invert or none the label ends "AT 1 IN 100 MIN." and the grade is a minimum; with both inverts given the grade is exact and no MIN. is printed. Automatic; it can still be ticked on or off on any pipe. (His own words on the practice: "MIN WAS SHOWN AT TIMES BECAUSE WE DESIGNED PIPES WITH INVERT LEVEL ON ONE END ONLY".)
18. **Invert level text (D-265):** "20TEXT, colour ByLayer (Recommended)".
19. **Label words by pipe kind (D-268):** three office wordings, charged, suspended and detention, each a line in the office list (full text on PIPE item 35); a better form for tight spaces is owed as a picture. **Answered by D-312 and D-367 (19 and 20 Nov):** "Always the full label"; too long for its pipe, stack on two lines first, then a callout (items 26 and 31).
20. **The APPROX. standing note (D-248):** "Yes, that wording (Recommended)". The note reads: "LEVELS MARKED APPROX. ARE ASSUMED AND ARE TO BE CONFIRMED ON SITE BY THE BUILDER PRIOR TO THE COMMENCEMENT OF WORK. NOTIFY THE ENGINEER OF ANY DIFFERENCE." It is a notes block on the Blocks palette, and the Unfinished list reminds the drafter to place it when anything prints APPROX.
21. **When a pit is turned its text does not (D-267):** "Text stays put and upright (Recommended)": only the pit box turns; the tag and cover level stay level with the sheet at the same offset from the pit centre. **To be re-read with him for the cover level (D-363, 20 Nov):** the cover level now sits at 45 degrees off a corner; see the INDEX clash list.
22. **The end of a system has no tag of its own (D-275):** it reads by kind in windows and lists, and is shown by a small marker on a CE no-plot layer (see OUTLET_AND_LPD item 12).
23. **The pipe label is text the Pipe tool writes, not an office label block (D-290; planning chat's call from his rulings; his to overturn):** see PIPE item 39. A typed-over wording is that text's own words, kept and flagged (D-182).
24. **Build calls on the pit's text (D-260; planning chat's call from his rulings; his to overturn) [D-260's DQ-009 offsets, number 900 above and cover level 700 below, superseded by D-363 and D-366 (20 Nov), item 30]:** DQ-004 plain text for numbers, levels and labels is allowed in tests only; before Tim drafts with a tool the pit number is the office's circled tag and the levels and labels are masked as the office draws them (D-039); DQ-008 plastic pit cover text as the office draws it, PP over CL on two lines, before first real use (D-039).
25. **Build calls on pipe and tank text (D-271, D-277, D-282; planning chat's call from his rulings; his to overturn):** DQ-027 a pipe's label moves with the pipe on a plain MOVE, keeping its offset, and a hand-moved label keeps its own (D-014, D-181); DQ-050 OVERTURNS the built answer: while a pipe's grade is not known the label prints TBC in its place (D-178), and while it is Wrong the label keeps the typed grade; DQ-066 the tank note is worded as the office writes it, no comma; DQ-067 commas in the windows, none on the sheet.

### Added 20 Nov 2026, D-298 to D-383

26. **Always the full label (D-312):** "Always the full label". The tool writes the full label every time; he moves it by hand where it clashes. The standing-note idea is not taken.
27. **Where the pipe label sits (D-361; pictures `label_positions_1.png` to `_4.png`):** "B but the text should also be able to move up and down the length of the pipe if clashign in the middle". Above the pipe, centred, 75 mm clear; on the open side if something is in the way; it slides along the pipe to clear a clash in the middle.
28. **Where the invert levels sit (D-362):** "the offset from the pit needs to be 200 and it needs to be a setting. inverts can be on opposite sides to each other if clashing". On the opposite side of the pipe to the label, 200 mm from the pit, a setting; one invert alone may flip sides, so the two ends may differ.
29. **The order text tries its spots (D-365, answering his "what if that clashes with something else" in D-362):** "Yes, that order (Recommended)". Pipe label: centred above, centred below, slid along above, slid along below. Each invert level: opposite the label 200 mm from the pit, then the label's side, then slid further along. Flip first, slide second.
30. **Where the pit's text sits (D-363, D-366):** "number straight above, below or side of pit and cover level always at 45 from any corner of pit", then "yes as drawn but distances need to be settings". Number: 900 mm above the pit centre first, then below, then beside (a side a pipe leaves from is skipped). Cover level: top right corner first, 400 mm off the corner, then round the pit, always at 45 degrees. The 900, the 400, the 75 mm label gap and the 200 mm invert offset are all SETTINGS. This supersedes D-260's draft call.
31. **A label longer than its pipe (D-367):** NOT DECIDED at first (TR-011: "callout option is great or we can keep centred but condense into multiple lines of text"; one line running past the pits is not wanted), then "both ideally, with stack as first option": two lines centred on the pipe first; a callout on a pointer when two lines still do not fit, and on any label by a grip. D-312 stands.
32. **When no spot is clear (D-368, not the recommended answer):** "First spot, and flag it". The text goes in its first spot on its mask and is flagged as overlapping; where that flag shows in Stage 1 is open (the Unfinished list is Stage 2; U-082).
33. **The flow arrow (D-369):** on every pipe, a tick turns it off: "Every pipe, tick to turn off (Recommended)". Where it sits: "centred on other side to pipe text, or centred to the text height alongside the pipe text if first option is not possible. you can also slide it along the length of the pipe also. if pipe label is off then arrow takes the pipe labels system". The pictures `flow_arrow_1.png`, `_2.png` show the first spot wrongly; his words supersede them.
34. **Bare pipes and bends (D-371):** the placing rules must also hold for a pipe with no label and with one invert level or none; how label, levels and arrow sit on a pipe with bends is not yet designed and blocks Stage 1 (U-107).
35. **Labels land in the right spot is a Stage 1 must (D-359):** "Labels land in the right spot,Grips to stretch and adjust,Levels work along the run".
36. **Every piece of text has its own tick (D-377 item 6):** a piece of text erased by hand stays gone, the same as unticking it on the part. Erasing a pipe takes its label, levels and arrow (D-377 item 5).
37. **MIRROR (D-381 item 8):** text stays readable and is re-placed by the placing rules; arrows still point with the flow.
38. **Inverts and MIN. (D-383 item 9):** every known invert is written, both ends (TR-065); the label ends "MIN." unless he typed both ends (TR-069).
