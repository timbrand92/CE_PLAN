Job window, first drawing (mock-up, made-up job)

What this window is for and what each part feeds

  1. Typed once per job. Step 1 holds the few job figures only you can give. Every tool reads them; change one here and everything follows.
  2. The panel at the left shows who reads it. Each tool in the chain lists the one or two job figures it takes, and waits (amber outline) if one of them is Not known.
  3. The office's standing figures are not typed here. Step 2 only shows them, each with its source. You change them in the office list.
  4. The drawing keeps what it was designed with. Step 3 records the office list of the day, so the job reads the same after the office list changes; you decide when to take up new figures.
