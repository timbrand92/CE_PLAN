## Clearing the three start-up lines yourself (optional; your file, your call)

**What CE does to AutoCAD's menus on its own (say it exactly like this to colleagues):** CE Manager loads its own menu file (`CE_SUITE_MANAGER_MENU.cuix`) and switches AutoCAD's classic menu bar ON (`MENUBAR` = 1) every time AutoCAD starts, even if you had turned it off (BUG-481). Do NOT tell colleagues that CE never touches their AutoCAD profile. The clean-up below is OPTIONAL: it edits YOUR own `acad.cuix` file and it is YOUR call, not something to hand to colleagues. If the "Menu item failed to load" lines bother you, you can clear them by hand. The desk can offer to run these steps on a COPY of your profile first with Codex. Nothing has been changed on your PC.

1. **Close AutoCAD.**
2. **Back up `acad.cuix`.** Find it with the folder shown under `CUI` > Customize > the main customisation file path, or search `%APPDATA%\Autodesk\AutoCAD 2027` for `acad.cuix`. Copy it beside itself as `acad.cuix.before_ce_cleanup`.
3. **Start AutoCAD**, type `CUI`, open the Customize tab.
4. **Find the CE entries.** In the "Customizations in All Files" tree, look under "Partial Customization Files" for the three CE entries named `CE_CVL_SUITE_MENU`, `CE_STRUC_SUITE_MENU` and `CE_SUITE_MANAGER_MENU`. Where an entry is missing on disk, right-click it and choose Unload (or Remove). Do not touch any other entry. (The start-up lines name the CVL and Manager ones; the STRUC one is on the desk's list of three.)
5. **Click Apply, then OK.**
6. **Close and restart AutoCAD** and read the start-up lines. The "Menu item failed to load" lines should be gone. The CE ribbon tabs are separate and stay.
7. **If anything looks wrong,** close AutoCAD and put the backup copy back over `acad.cuix`.

**Read first, for the other line ("; error: syntax error").** Its source is NOT found. The places to read (read only) are `acad.lsp` and `acaddoc.lsp` in the AutoCAD support path, and any startup suite. Nothing has been changed. The steps above do not touch it, so expect that line to stay.
