# Typed names: the rule (decided) and the list (draft)

## The rule (Tim, 16 Nov 2026: D-052 to D-056)

1. **Two names each, the way AutoCAD's own commands work.** A plain full word, and a short name of one to three letters (Copy and C, Trim and T).
2. **Short name = first letters of the word.** One letter if free, otherwise two or three.
3. **One office list** holds the short names, the same kind of list the office keeps today (`evidence\TIM_ALIAS_LIST_16NOV.md`), so the office can change one.
4. **Only placing a thing or opening a sheet job has a typed name.** Changing a thing is done by clicking it (tab, grips, Properties).
5. **Old names are gone** from day one.
6. **IO stays; C and RC are renamed.**
7. Every ribbon button shows its short name in its tip (R-001 Borrow).

## How a new name is checked

1. Against AutoCAD 2027's 241 standard short names (R-001 section E item 8).
2. Against the office's own list (`evidence\TIM_ALIAS_LIST_16NOV.md`).
3. Typed once into AutoCAD 2027 to prove the full word is not an AutoCAD command: **to be proven in Stage 4** for every name below.

## Draft list (checked against 1 and 2 only; not yet shown to Tim as final)

Things you place:

1. **Pit:** PIT, short PT.
2. **Pipe:** PIPE, short PI. (One name; the kind of pipe is its first choice. Not yet ruled.)
3. **Downpipe:** DOWNPIPE, short DP.
4. **Overflow:** OVERFLOW, short OF.
5. **FO:** short FO. Full word: Unknown (what the office calls it in full has not been asked).
6. **Inspection opening:** INSPECTION, short IO (kept by D-054; sits on AutoCAD's INSERTOBJ short name).
7. **Grated inspection opening:** short GIO (already what is written on the drawing). Full word: to ask.
8. **Trench grate:** TRENCHGRATE, short TG.
9. **Floor waste:** FLOORWASTE, short FW.
10. **Tank:** TANK, short TK. (WT is the office's wall tool; TA is Text Align.)
11. **AG drain:** AGDRAIN, short AG.
12. **Slab:** SLAB, short SB. (S, SA, SC, SS, ST and SL are taken.)

Sheet jobs:

13. **Job:** JOB (already three letters; J is Join).
14. **Levels:** LEVELS, short LV.
15. **Pit schedule:** SCHEDULE, short SH.
16. **Check:** CHECK, short CK. (CH is Chamfer; CHK is AutoCAD's.)
17. **Issue:** ISSUE, short IS.

Everyday helpers (CE tools): not yet listed. C and RC need new names (D-054). Q, MR, CR, QD, LD, AD and the rest to be gone through with Tim when CE tools are designed.
