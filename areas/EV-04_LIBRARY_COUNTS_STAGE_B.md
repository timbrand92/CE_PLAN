## 1. How good these numbers are

1. **Sheet titles come from the title block.** 7736 of 11063 sheets (70%) gave a readable title. Sheets without one count as "not found", never guessed.
2. **Job types are keyword estimates** from Evidence Job 2, mapped onto the 13 types Tim confirmed on 30 Oct. Jobs whose PDFs had no readable text are kept as their own group.
3. **A sheet can count for more than one kind**, and "drawn inside a sheet" means the heading was found on the sheet whatever its title.
4. **Construction methods stay Unknown,** for the reason in stage A section 5: footing plans name several systems in their standard legends.
