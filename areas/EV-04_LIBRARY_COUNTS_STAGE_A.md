## 1. Read this first: how good these numbers are

1. **The copied drawings and PDFs are gone.** The brief said they were still in `C:\ce_evidence_local\job2\`; the working folder is not there. Only the saved page text survived, so stage A ran on that.
2. **Page text was saved cut short.** 583 of 889 sheets were cut at 3,500 characters. On most sheets the title block is past the cut, so the true sheet title is usually missing.
3. **So sheet kinds are counted from the view titles printed under each plan or detail** (for example "ROOF FRAMING PLAN", "FOOTING DETAILS - SHEET 2"). A kind that appears only in a title block is missed. Counts are a floor, not exact.
4. **Job types are keyword estimates** from Evidence Job 2, mapped one to one onto the 13 types Tim confirmed on 30 Oct.
5. **Construction methods are NOT usable yet.** Plan sheets carry standard legends and notes that name nearly every system (piers, stumps, rafters, trusses, portals) whether used or not. The counts below are "mentioned on the sheet", not "used on the job".
6. **Discipline** comes from the issue folder name (structural, civil, or a combined folder). In a combined folder each sheet is sorted by its titles.

## 7. What could not be read, and why

1. **583 of 889 sheets cut at 3,500 characters** when Evidence Job 2 saved them. Title blocks mostly lost.
2. **20 sheets had no text at all** (scanned or picture-only PDFs), across 5 jobs. No job was entirely blank.
3. **2 jobs with a structural set and 4 with a civil set showed no recognisable view title** in the saved text. They are in the "of" totals and count as not found.
4. **No PDF failed to open** in Evidence Job 2 for these 78 jobs. Zero silent skips: every job is in every total above.
5. **Drawing (DWG) title block attributes were not saved** by Evidence Job 2 (5 of 1,341 layouts had any), so drawings could not stand in for the missing titles.

## 8. Recommendation before stage B

Re-read the issued PDFs of these same 78 jobs, read only, with the full page text and the title block corner kept (my estimate 10 to 30 minutes, no AutoCAD needed). That gives true sheet titles and lets the method counts separate "used" from "mentioned". Stage B should use the same reader.
