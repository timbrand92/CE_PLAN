How the office draws the preview: Known, Assumed, UNKNOWN

Everything in the preview comes from the files in C:\CE_PLAN\zero\reference. Where they are silent it says UNKNOWN; nothing was invented to fill a gap without being listed here.

Known

  1. New pipe: layer STORMWATER PIPE, colour 150, DASHED, a fat polyline as wide as the pipe, plus two thin wall lines on layer DRAINAGE, colour 6 (layers.csv lines 20 and 25; ELEMENTS.md E-001, line 47).
  2. Dash 500 and gap 250 on the ground (linetypes.csv line 2). Detention pipe: colour 160 on STORMWATER DETENTION PIPE, from the brief; the layer is still a placeholder in layers.csv line 148.
  3. Pit symbol: layer STORMWATER, colour 9; outline on PITS HIDDEN, colour 7, HIDDEN; cross lines or grate hatch forced to colour 9 (layers.csv lines 31 to 34; E-008, line 161).
  4. Pipe label: layer 25TEXT, colour 7, 250 high, top centre, masked; wording "<dia> DIA. <material> PIPE AT 1 IN <grade>" (E-006, lines 126-127; wording_patterns.csv line 2; text_heights.csv line 2).
  5. Text style STANDARD, Arial Narrow, for everything (ELEMENTS.md line 31).
  6. Invert text: red (colour 10 on 20TEXT, or colour 1 on 18TEXT), 180 high, "IL 5.28" (E-014, lines 260-261). Cover text: 25TEXT, colour 7, 200 high, turned 45 degrees, "CL 5.83" (E-013, lines 245-246).
  7. Flow arrow: 25DIMS, colour 7, leader 750 long, filled head 300 long (E-007, line 147).
  8. Pit tag: 35TEXT, colour 2 (yellow), 350 high, in a circle mask on 50CONT (E-012, line 230; layers.csv lines 12, 18).
  9. Downpipe: circle colour 2, red fill colour 10, "DP" text colour 7, 250 high (E-015, lines 275-276).
  10. House outline: 50CONT, colour 3, width 70; footprint fill colour 149 in model space (layers.csv lines 18 and 54). Boundary: colour 2, DASHDOT (layers.csv line 74).
  11. Pipes stop at the pit's solid inner wall line, not the dashed outer line (D-102, E-001 line 56).

Assumed

  1. Screen background #212830: AutoCAD's own default dark model-space colour. The office's own setting is not in the reference.
  2. AutoCAD index colours drawn at their standard RGB: 150 = #007FFF, 160 = #003FFF, 6 = #FF00FF, 9 = #C0C0C0, 10 = #FF0000, 2 = #FFFF00, 3 = #00FF00, 149 = #26394C.
  3. Preview scale: 1 mm of ground = 0.1 px, so text heights keep their real proportions to the pipe.
  4. Pit outer dashed square: 583 for the 350 pit (measured on one copy), scaled up for the 450 pits (E-008 item 4, line 163).
  5. Label above the pipe, invert text above at each end, flow arrow below, cover text turned 45 degrees: all inside the measured ranges, none a fixed rule (label_offsets.csv).
  6. Pit P4 as a 350 grated pit, P3 as a 450 junction pit, P2 as a 450 grated pit: made up to show both symbols.
  7. The CE square mark is my own drawing; the brand files hold the wordmark only.

UNKNOWN

  1. UNKNOWN: which layer and colour the pit's solid inner wall line is on. E-008 says it exists; I drew it colour 9.
  2. UNKNOWN: the size and colour of the pit tag circle. I drew a green circle (the layer 50CONT colour) with yellow text.
  3. UNKNOWN: how the office's detention pit symbol looks (only the 600x1200 state name is known).
  4. UNKNOWN: which side of the pipe the flow arrow normally sits on (the rows say either side).
  5. UNKNOWN: how many characters of the label fit before the office moves it to a leader label.
  6. UNKNOWN: what the office's screen background and linework preferences are on each machine.
  7. UNKNOWN: whether Arial Narrow exists on every colleague's PC (the page falls back to Arial).
  8. UNKNOWN: a locator view in AutoCAD itself: how it is produced is a Stage 4 trial (UI_PLAN section 9, item 3).
