# B-029 BUG-1096 with BUG-1098: Rainwater reuse boxes in CDDESIGN and the Kingston credit (Fri 30 Oct 2026)

## FOR TIM (plain English, read this and stop)

1. **The pull request is #1874 (draft).** https://github.com/timbrand92/CE_ACAD_PLUGIN/pull/1874. Built and unit-tested only. Nobody has opened the window in AutoCAD. BUG-1096 and BUG-1098 stay open.
2. **What you will see in CDDESIGN.** On the "Areas and route" page there is a new "Rainwater reuse" box group on the right: tank size in litres, roof connected to the tank (filled in for you when the rating report lists a tank) and a "Plumbed for reuse" tick. Leave the tank size blank and nothing changes.
3. **What changes on the result.** Type a tank and the result gets a "RAINWATER-TANK REUSE CREDIT" section: gross storage, credit and net. The credit is shown beside the gross storage; the design is never reduced by it.
4. **Kingston no longer over-credits.** A 6,000 L tank now credits 1.00 m3 (it credited 1.50 m3). A 2,500 L tank credits 1.00 m3 (it credited 0.50 m3). A 2,000 L tank credits 0.50 m3. Under 2,000 L of tank, or under 50 m2 of roof, there is no credit and the sheet says why.
5. **A council with no recorded reuse rule** (for example Frankston or Darebin) now says "NEEDS CONFIRMATION (Unknown) - no credit taken" and one "Watch out:" line. It never shows a quiet zero.
6. **THE BIG LIMIT, said plainly: your Manningham example cannot appear in CDDESIGN yet.** Manningham, Kingston, Monash, Bayside and Casey are councils that hand us the permitted discharge, and CDDESIGN has no box to type it in. So on those councils CDDESIGN has no gross storage figure to take a credit from. It now says so once ("Reuse tank entered, but there is no detention storage figure to credit yet") instead of staying silent. The 10 m3 gross, 300 m2 roof, 3,000 L plumbed tank example (credit 1.50 m3, net 8.50 m3) is proved by the unit tests through the design engine with a discharge supplied. **Decision for you (CE 04 to ask):** may CDDESIGN get one more optional box for the council's permitted discharge (or storage) so the credit shows on those councils? Recommendation: yes, it is the only way your example works on a real Manningham job.
7. **Switch:** `detention.rainwaterReuseBoxes`, ON. OFF hides the group, takes no credit and puts Kingston back on the old rule, exactly as before.
8. **Not done:** the three boxes are not saved in the drawing (typed again each time CDDESIGN opens); Manningham's "majority of the tank must be reuse" check (BUG-1108) needs a detention-litres box; Kingston's "single dwelling, own tank" is not asked (the sheet notes it); the saved answer for BUG-1100's question is not built.
