## How to read this coverage map

The 47 XAML surfaces and 40 code-created surface groups in the retained-control ledger are reconciled by **user action**, not by a count of fields/buttons. A surface is covered only where an action contract names its selection, state change, preview/confirmation or recovery boundary. “Root” means a named shared owner has the complete contract; it does not mean the source route may disappear. “Native-only” means source cannot prove the interactive AutoCAD result. “Tim decision” means an engineering/product choice is not settled in the records read.
