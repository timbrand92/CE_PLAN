# Waffle slabs, timber subfloors, screw piles, suspended/basement slabs — CE practice (DRAFT, 26 Sep 2026)

Read-only study approved by Tim (UX-FDN-05): 4 structural sets read in full (2021-2025) and ~140 keyword-scanned
(2021-2026). Anonymised (DATA-01). **Items 1-4 CONFIRMED by Tim, 26 Sep 2026** (short questions; decisions file). Item 5 unverified.

1. **Waffle pod slabs:** 85-150 mm slab, mesh top (and bottom), 300 mm pods (225 locally), min rib 110 mm with
   1-N12 top and bottom, extra bars per rib-width tables; tags EB/IB/TH/PF/SF/C, screw piles SP1-SP4 when piled;
   Footing Schedule (type, min width, min depth, reinforcement); legend plus a numbered "WAFFLE POD RAFT SLAB NOTES"
   block; footing plan plus 1-2 footing-detail sheets; "CE Residential Waffle Slab Details".
2. **Timber subfloors:** traditional stumps/bearers/joists or SHS steel stumps on concrete pads/piers; tags BR1/BR2
   bearers, GFJ1/DJ1 joists, DB1/DB2 deck bearers, DS1/TS1 at supports, FB floor beams; a Member Schedule (mark, size)
   grouped "Sub-Floor Framing" and "Porch/Deck Floor Framing"; notes on AS 1684 clearance, AS 2870 backfill,
   embedment, joist hangers; "CE Timber Sub Floor Details" and "CE Standard Framing Details".
3. **Screw piles:** SP1-SP4 by design load at rib/beam intersections; "SCREW PILE SCHEDULE (SAFE WORKING LOADS)"
   (type, design load kN); supplier certification note; geotechnical founding note; no in-house standard detail
   found.
4. **Suspended/basement slabs:** bondek (200-270 mm, mesh top and bottom, f'c 32) over band beams BB1/BB2, or in-situ
   suspended raft over soldier-pile/shotcrete walls; tags BW, RTW, CB1, SPW1, C; schedules: Basement Footing,
   Soldier Pile Retaining Wall, Blockwork Retaining Wall; band-beam penetration note; construction-sequence and
   propping notes; separate Basement Footing and Retention Plan and Ground Floor Slab Plan; details in "Retention and
   Suspended Slab Details".
5. **Unverified:** screw-pile and band-beam standard details; whether BSSLAB (registry "Phase 3") works.
