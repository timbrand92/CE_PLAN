# B-057 report: the Grade mismatch window on short pipes (Fri 30 Oct 2026)

## FOR TIM

1. **PR:** draft PR #1873 (branch fb/b057-mismatch-box-own-suggestion, against claude/rel-20261030a). Built and unit-tested only. Nobody has opened it in AutoCAD.
2. **What you will see differently:** draw a short pipe (say 4.1 m) with "Set my own" 1 in 100 and "I know one end", type the upstream level 19.45, leave the downstream level CE worked out (19.40) and press Draw. The "Grade mismatch" window no longer pops up. Same for 6.1 m at 1 in 200 and 2.5 m at 1 in 100. The pipe label still says 1 IN 100 (the grade you typed).
3. **What still pops up:** if you type or change the downstream level yourself and it is more than 10 percent off your grade, the window still opens, exactly as today.
4. **Why it happened:** the earlier fix (#1838) makes CE round the suggested level DOWN so the pipe is never flatter than your grade. On a short pipe that rounds a lot: 4.1 m at 1 in 100 comes out at 1 in 82, 18 percent steeper, and the window treated that as a mismatch.
5. **Switch:** Settings key pipe.mismatchBoxSkipsOwnSuggestion, on by default. Off gives today's windows exactly. There is no row for it on the Settings page (the other C-77 switches have none either).
6. **Question for CE 04 (your call, not decided):** when the window is skipped, the label says the grade you TYPED (1 IN 100), not the real 1 in 82. That is the label rule this pipe already used when you pick "keep typed grade". Should the label show the real grade instead when CE worked the level out? Example: 4.1 m, label 1 IN 100 or 1 IN 82?
