How I want every screen designed (UI/UX rules — follow them exactly)

WHO I AM
- I'm a visual thinker. I read pages like a maths teacher: numbers,
  sums, charts and diagrams, lined up vertically and horizontally.
- I have no coding knowledge. Show me, don't describe. Before building
  any screen, show me a clickable mock-up or picture and wait for my OK.

LOOK AND FEEL
- Apple-like: clean, calm, lots of space, rounded cards, no clutter.
- One consistent design language across every page. Build a small set
  of shared parts (card, list, button, field, status pill, chart) first
  and build every page from them.
- One brand accent colour, used only for brand things: main buttons and
  the selected item. Never for data.
- Dark and light mode both designed properly. No black panels next to
  white backgrounds.
- Must work on regular iPhone, a folding phone (folded and unfolded),
  iPad and desktop. Check every screen at all of them.

CARDS — HOW EACH ONE READS
1. One answer per card: one biggest, boldest number.
2. Order: short label → answer → working → small grey note.
3. Show the working as a sum: + and − in their own column, figures
   right-aligned, a line above the result, a double line under the final
   answer.
4. Three text sizes only: answer, working, note.
5. Bold the number, not the label. At most one bold item per line.
6. Every card shows its answer visually — a chart, sum, tally or image.
   Cards of words alone don't work for me.
7. Squint test: blurred, each card still shows its answer and any
   warning, and nothing else.

COLOUR
- Colour must mean something, or it isn't used. Always pair it with a
  word, never colour alone.
- Green = good, amber = needs me, red = late / over / wrong. Status only.
- Charts: up to 5 distinct series colours in a fixed order used
  everywhere (e.g. blue → teal → violet → pink → slate), separate
  shades for dark and light mode, contrast-checked. More than 5 → group
  the rest as "Other". Label bars and lines directly, not in a legend.

FEWER TAPS
- Every common task has a tap budget (e.g. approve something: 1 tap).
  Write it down and test it; over budget = not finished.
- Pre-fill from where I am and from last time.
- Let AI do the typing (email, photo, voice → a draft; one tap to save).
- Show the few fields that matter; the rest under "More".
- Undo instead of "Are you sure?".
- Actions on the card, without opening the record.
- Start from "what needs me" — a home screen of what's urgent, ranked.
- On small screens, use icons on small buttons where words don't fit.
- Smooth zoom-in transitions when drilling from overview to detail.

WORDS
- Every word must be understandable by a tradie or a regular person.
  Short sentences, everyday words, no IT words ("sync", "error 500").
- Errors say what happened and what to do.
- Formal or accounting terms only in brackets after the plain word.
- Short labels, not questions, as titles.

HOW TO WORK WITH ME
- Answer first, then detail only if it changes what I do.
- Ask me with clickable choices, one recommended, each with a
  real-world example of what it would mean.
- Tell me exactly what to click to test it, and what "right" looks like.
- Never say it's done until you've looked at the real screen yourself.
  I'm the only one who says it's accepted.
