LESSONS FROM THIS WEEK (each one cost a robot run; avoid them).
1. A pipe typed flatter than 1 in 100 over its length opens the Grade mismatch window on 3.0.87 and later, and while it is open every key is refused. Use SETUP-R47v / P8v / P8x2v, or type levels that give 1 in 100 or steeper (10 m of pipe: fall of at least 0.100; 20 m: 0.200). Re-do the arithmetic for YOUR pipe length: a level map made for 20 m is wrong for a 10 m pipe (R39v87 failed twice for this).
2. After a STRETCH or MOVE the end level follows the longer or shorter pipe at the same grade (a 20 m pipe 18.000 to 17.800 stretched 2 m reads IL 17.78, not 17.800).
3. CE moves each pipe end 225 mm into the pit wall: a pit-to-pit length is wall to wall, 450 mm shorter than centre to centre when both ends are on 450 pits.
4. 'ent-read' needs a HANDLE; read it first with 'field $list handle'. 'field' on an empty selection fails the step. After commands that redraw a whole network (FPFC and the like) the new objects can be regions, not polylines: record the new objects first.
5. The driver refuses the word PRINT, SAVE, QSAVE, OPEN, NETLOAD and the like: in any Lisp use princ / vl-prin1-to-string.
6. A CE window left open (New Pipe, New Pit, Grade mismatch, Edit source) disables COM: every case must close it with a known press or Esc before the next step. After Edit Pipe or CD, wait for the window to close before a pointer step.
7. A pass needs TWO separate AutoCAD starts: the runner does that itself (pass a, pass b); a case with a SETTING line runs in its own guarded start. Never leave Tim's real settings changed: use SETTING lines (the guard puts the files back).
8. The first CDRG on the standard fixture prints RG2 (the drawing holds RG1). Fixtures: DRV1_B1 (Civil, pits P1.. with pipes, the one most cases use). State in your report what you read from it; do not guess names or counts.
9. The twin must break ONE thing and fail on a CHECK (an assertion), never on a stalled step. A twin that fails because the case could not start proves nothing.
10. Do not copy a street name, client name or job address into any file: made-up drawings only, job numbers only.
