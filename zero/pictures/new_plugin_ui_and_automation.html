Numbered in the order of how often each is used, most first. That order is my design intent, not a measurement. The card and the notice are drawn in so all seven can be seen at once; they are not open all the time. Help comes in 5 rungs, further down this page.

  1. Where the side panel docks.

    On the right, like Properties (recommended: the eye already goes there) · on the left · floating.

  2. How a label flips with its grip.

    Drag it across the pipe (recommended: no new thing to learn) · right-click only · a small menu on the grip.

  3. What double-click opens.

    The small card (recommended) · today's large edit window · the side panel.

  4. Is "P2 too deep for its size" red or amber.

    Red, because a standard is exceeded (recommended) · amber, because the engineer may accept it.

  5. "Leave as is".

    A short typed reason, kept with the drawing (recommended, your ruling on disagreements) · pick a reason from a list · no reason needed.

  6. A suggestion you said "Not now" to.

    One quiet line stays on Checks (recommended) · gone for good · asked again at Issue.

  7. Can Issue run with a red line on Checks.

    No, not until it is fixed or a reason is written (recommended, as the concept says) · yes, with a warning.

  8. Where "What CE did" lives.

    At the foot of the Checks tab (recommended) · its own sixth tab · a separate window.

  9. Where the standards list opens from.

    One small button at the top of the side panel (recommended: out of the way) · a ribbon button · a typed name only.

  10. The 20 ribbon buttons.

    My split is Job 3, Network 7, Levels 3, Sheet 4, Check 3, with Inlet opening to IO, OF, GIO and floor waste (recommended as a start) · fewer, with more behind drop-downs · you mark up the list.

  11. The strip while something is selected.

    Its own short tab beside CE that shows and goes (recommended, as AutoCAD does for hatches) · an extra panel at the end of the CE tab.

  12. The start-up notice.

    One small notice in the corner, once per version (recommended) · a window in the middle of the screen · only inside the Manager.
