Rainwater tanks drawn true to size

Tim: "we have in the past not shown water tanks to scale we need to create a block or water tank that is correct to size".

Everything on this page is drawn to ONE scale. Each white picture has its own 1 m scale bar, and a boundary with a 900 mm side setback strip (the grey hatched band). Each tank is pushed up against the boundary so its width can be compared with the strip.

Sizes are the middle of the ranges in research note R-015. Every slimline and steel figure in R-015 is "still not checked" at source (no office drawing for these makers), so each says "typical, confirm with maker". Plan view, long side horizontal. Height is only in the label.

1. Slimline tanks

2,000, 3,000, 3,500 and 5,000 litres. There is no checked size for 3,500 L, so nothing is drawn for it.

Where the sizes came from (R-015, section 1): 2,000 L from 2000 to 2400 long, 565 to 625 wide; 3,000 L from 2400 to 3010 long, 650 to 810 wide; 5,000 L from 2900 to 3290 long, 940 to 1080 wide. Some of these figures are search snippets only.

2. Round tanks

2,000, 5,000 and 10,000 litres. Diameter is drawn, height is in the label.

The 2,000 and 5,000 L round figures are small round steel tanks (nominal sizes, one maker, R-015 section 3). The 10,000 L figure is round poly, from 2480 to 2600 across and 2100 to 2380 high (some snippets only). R-015 found no round 2,500 or 3,500 L at all.

3. The two outlines the office used before

Dashed, with no real tank behind them. On the right, each is laid over a real typical size (solid) from the same top corner.

How should the tool draw a tank?

Three ways. Each card shows the label that would sit beside the tank on the plan. The tank shown is the 3,000 L slimline.

The label example Tim gave read "3,500 L ... 2250 x 800 x 2100". R-015 has no checked 3,500 L size, so these cards use the 3,000 L figure instead.

A
Pick a maker preset by volume, outline drawn to those sizes
What the drafter doesPicks 3,000 L from a list. Nothing to type.
Watch outIf the real tank is a different make, the outline is wrong and there is no way to fix it in the tool.
B
Type length and width (or diameter); the tool shows the volume that gives at the typed height
What the drafter doesTypes the maker's three sizes.
Watch outA plain box gives 4,200 L for a tank the maker rates at 3,000 L, about 40% high. The litres on the label would mislead.
C
Both: presets fill the boxes, typing over them wins
Recommended

Why: the preset sizes are only typical (R-015), so the drafter must be able to type the maker's real size on the day. A preset still saves the typing, and it keeps the maker's rated litres on the label.

What the drafter doesPicks 3,000 L, or types straight over any box. Typed sizes win and the litres drop off the label.
Watch outThe label must say TYPED when the preset has been changed, so a builder knows the size is not a catalogue figure.

Tank sizes are typical figures from R-015 and are not checked at source. Nothing here is built into the tool.
