The new plugin, designed from scratch

My interpretation, drawn from your decisions of 16 Nov and the civil study. Draft 1, not approved. Pictures are mock-ups with made-up but realistic numbers. How it is built inside is not decided here; that is the research stage.

The idea

The drawing holds one drainage network. Every label, level, schedule row, criteria line and detail number is a view of it. Each part still drags on its own. Standards live in one place. What prints is ordinary AutoCAD.

1. One element, parts still editable

Today

13 loose objects for one pipe run and its two pits. Move the pit and the label, levels, tag and schedule stay behind.

New

1 element. Click any part and the run lights up. Drag the pit and everything tied to it comes along. Drag just the label and only the label moves.

Label distance from the pipe comes from the standards (0.75 mm on paper). A label you slid for a clash stays put and shows a small mark; "reset to standard" puts it back.

  2. On release: inverts are untouched. Labels re-read the true grade ("1 IN 96"). Anything now under a limit goes amber on the plan and on Checks.

7. The sheet fills itself

Read from the network

  1. Pit schedule (a pit with two inlets gets its two rows).
  2. Detention criteria box.
  3. Roof-to-tank areas box.
  4. The job's orifice pit detail: cover, top water level, baffle, orifice, inverts.
  5. Tank detail: litres, split, 30 orifice.
  6. Computations PDF.

Read from the job facts

  1. Title block, drawing register, revision rows.
  2. Issue stamp for the stage picked at Issue.
  3. Council notes block for that council.
  4. "Assumed, builder to confirm" wording where a fact is still assumed.

Placed by name from the office library

  1. Standard details (pipe bedding, junction pit, penetrations).
  2. General notes, legend, disclaimer, warning boxes.

The parked kit of 600 samples beside the sheet goes away.

8. With no plugin: the hard promise

Your live case is the test. Today a drawing made with the present plugin shows nothing to people without it. In this design everything that prints is an ordinary AutoCAD object (polyline, text, block), exactly what your hand-drawn sheets are made of now. The tie between parts is hidden data that an outsider's AutoCAD simply ignores.

Proof, every release

Open the sheet on a PC with nothing installed. Plot it. Compare with the office plot. Must be identical.

If an outsider edits it

They can; it is ordinary linework to them. When it comes back, the plugin says what no longer matches. How much of the tie survives is a research question.

Honest limit

I have not yet proven how best to do this inside AutoCAD 2027. It is the first thing the research stage must settle, with your live drawing as a case.

10. Built to be added to

Every kind of thing is described the same six ways, so a new kind is added without touching the old ones.

1 Facts it holdsEach with a source and a status.
2 Parts it showsOrdinary objects at standard positions.
3 What it connects toFrom, to, feeds, overflows to.
4 Sums it feedsStorage, schedule, checks.
5 Where else it appearsSchedule, boxes, details.
6 Its checksWith the limit's source.

Five statuses for a fact, and no more: given, measured, chosen, worked out, assumed.

11. Order of building (no dates)

Release 1One real house or units job, start to issue: job facts, pits, pipes, symbols, tanks, levels, checks, schedule, title and issue, the no-plugin proof, one-click update, CE Tools.

Each tool gets its own interview, design record and mock-up before anything is built. Nothing here is automatic design: it shows, checks and carries; the engineer designs.
