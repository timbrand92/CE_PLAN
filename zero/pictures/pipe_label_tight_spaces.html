Pipe labels in tight spaces: four ways

The full label is about 6.8 m long on a 1:100 plan, longer than most of the 4 m pipes it describes. Same bit of plan in all four, drawn as AutoCAD shows it: a 1.5 m side passage between the house wall and the boundary fence.

What I would do

  1. Standing note by default. One note on the sheet for the ordinary pipes, full labels only on the exceptions.
  2. Two-line stack as a click. Offered per label, for the tight spots.
  3. Full one-line label always available. Your office wording, for anyone who wants it on every pipe.

Your wording is unchanged in all four; only how much of it is drawn changes.
