# Monash — Engineering Plan Checklist

**15 September 2026.** Source: *Engineering Plan Checklist*, City of Monash, obtained free from the council's own
site. (Tim sent a Scribd copy first; that is paywalled at page 1 of 5 — the council publishes it openly, so
**always take the council's own copy**.)

**This is the most directly checkable council document read so far.** It is a signed declaration — the designer
completes it, signs it, and submits it with the plans — so every line is something a reviewer will look for.

---

## 1. Real numbers, not guidance

| Clause | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **4.3** | Orifice diameter **not less than 65 mm** |
| **4.4** | **OSD stored above ground must not exceed 40%** of total OSD volume |
| **6.5** | Minimum cover for all pipes **300 mm** (recommended) |
| **6.6** | All pipes, particularly under driveways, **minimum 100 mm UPVC, ideally 150 mm** |
| **6.11** | Kerb and channel connection at least **1 m from any vehicle crossing kerb return** |
| **6.12** | **Only ONE connection point** into Council's drainage system |
| **7.1** | Tanks drawn to scale, **500 mm minimum clearance to one side**, grated pit adjacent, not impacting windows or doorways |
| **7.4** | Tank orifices at **approximately two-thirds of tank height** from the base |
| **8.2** | Pavement **1 in 40 cross fall** to a central invert, **minimum 1:200 longitudinal**, per AS2890 |
| **8.4** | Trench grates at the boundary **minimum 300 mm internal width**, behind the boundary not in the road reserve |
| **2.2** | Spot levels at **all property corners and at 10 m intervals** on all boundaries |
| **2.6** | Existing contours at **0.2 m** on flat sites, **0.5 m** otherwise |
| **3.5** | Retaining wall top and toe levels at **3 m intervals** |
| **8.1** | Pavement levels to edges and invert at **3–5 m spacing** |

**4.4 confirms the guidebook.** CE's guidebook records "minimum 60% underground detention" for Monash; the
checklist states the same rule from the other side — **no more than 40% above ground**. Two independent sources
agreeing is worth having.

**7.4 is new and useful.** Nothing in CE's material says where a tank orifice sits. Monash wants it at about
**two-thirds of the tank height** — which is a direct statement of the re-use/detention split Tim ruled on
(re-use below, detention above, orifice at the change), expressed as a height rather than a volume.

## 2. Rules the plugin can check directly

These need no judgement — the drawing either has them or it does not:

- **6.4** every pit shown, **numbered**, with a **pit schedule** giving type, material, dimensions and all levels
- **6.1** pipe sizes, material, grades and all levels shown; **all downpipes and house drains**, including on
  existing dwellings
- **6.3** no pipe bends or junctions under pavements without an **inspection opening or pit** per AS3500
- **6.8** a drainage pit for **each unit at each low point** of private open space and service yards
- **4.2** no detention storage, charged pipes or pits **under any structure or within easements**
- **3.3** total **impervious area** shown in m²; all impervious areas shown including rooflines, paving, driveways
- **7.5 / 7.6** roof area directed to each tank **and** roof area not directed to a tank, both in m²
- **2.3** title boundaries with lengths and heading; **total site area in m²**
- **6.13** the outfall drain to Council must be **gravity fed**
- **5.2** a basement's detention system **separate from** the property's

## 3. Rules about consistency with the planning permit

- **3.1** finished floor levels shown, complying with the **Condition 1 approved plans**, and where required above
  the **1% AEP surcharge level with adequate freeboard**
- **3.3** site layout on the drainage plan **matches the endorsed Condition 1 plans**
- **3.6** only structures shown on the Condition 1 / Secondary Consent plans are permitted over easements

Same pattern as Kingston: **the drainage plan must agree with the endorsed architectural plans**. Three councils
now require this. It is a drawing-against-drawing check and currently done by eye.

## 4. Basements — stricter than expected

- **5.1** basement detention for events **up to 1% AEP**
- **5.2** basement detention **separated from** the property detention system
- **5.3** a **back-up diesel pump**, raised off the basement floor, to work while the basement is flooded
- **5.4** pump and pump pit details shown

The diesel back-up requirement is not in CE's guidebook and is a real design obligation on any Monash basement
job.

## 5. Presentation rules that will catch people out

- **1.6** plans drawn **in black lines** — no greyscale, no colour
- **6.10** pits other than Council's are **per AS3500 and must not be circular**
- **6.9** grated pits in trafficked areas fitted with **"Bike Safe" grates**
- **2.5** north point, **up the page preferred**
- **1.5** designer's name, **qualifications** and contact details shown
- **9.2** a copy of the drainage computations provided

## 6. What this changes

1. **A signed checklist is a specification.** Every line is a pass/fail a reviewer applies. This is the clearest
   case yet for a "ready to submit?" check built from the council's own list.
2. **Monash's PSD and OSD still come from the council** (4.1 — "provided by the City of Monash"), confirming CE's
   guidebook. The checklist governs the *design*, not the *parameters*.
3. **Tank orifice height** (two-thirds) is a new, concrete rule worth holding.
4. **"No more than 40% above ground"** cross-checks the guidebook's 60% underground.

## 7. Questions

1. **The "Bike Safe" grate requirement** (6.9) — does CE specify these on Monash jobs today, and are they on the
   standard pit blocks?
2. **Pits must not be circular** (6.10) — does that rule out any pit CE currently uses?
3. **The back-up diesel pump** in basements (5.3) — is that being specified on Monash basement jobs?
4. **Only one connection point** into Council's system (6.12) — does CE ever design two, and would that be
   refused?
