**Source.** Tim pointed the planning chat at the office standard details drawing for water tanks and pump stations (in the office Drafting folder, Standard Details, Civil) on 19 Nov 2026 at about 9:50 am. The planning chat read only the titles and size notes written on the details (137 lines of text), with a small reading script; nothing was changed. Sizes are as the office wrote them on its details, so they are the makers' figures as the office last copied them: still "confirm with maker" on a sheet. Length x width x height in millimetres. No job is named here.

**How this is used (planning chat's reading of why he sent it, his to correct).** This list is the tank preset list for the Tank window (D-335): picking one fills the sizes; typing over wins; the litres are always the stated figure. Above-ground slimline tanks are drawn in plan with fully rounded ends (D-336). The plan shape of the other kinds (underground flat, underdeck, torpedo) is to be taken from the office's own detail for that tank, not guessed.
