**Tim's answer, 23 Sep 2026 (after asking "explain more first"):** *"Live is the trunk"*.

From here every merge to master reaches the bundle at the next deploy, and register #78's rule "branch from LIVE, not from master" becomes "branch from master". Nothing in AutoCAD changes until the next deploy on Tim's phrase.
