# R-019 Bends and inspection openings: what the standard and the office say

**Written:** Fri 20 Nov 2026, evening (PC clock), by Tim's room. **Feeds:** U-098 ("however many bends are required within the standards", TR-005); U-041 (run length before an inspection opening).

**What was checked and what was not.** Checked at source by Tim's room: the office council checklist (a Word document) for the wording "Pipe bends other than 45 deg to have inspection openings (IO)". Everything else below was read by a research helper on the Team account and has NOT been re-read by the room, including every clause of the plumbing standard quoted here. Sources are named in words; no web addresses. Not yet put to Tim.

## The question
What bends are allowed in a buried stormwater pipe, and when is an inspection opening (IO) needed?

## What was found
1. **Where an IO is required (standard).** AS/NZS 3500.3:2018, clause 7.4.1 (PDF page 82, printed page 80): "For other than single dwellings, inspection openings for the maintenance of site stormwater drains shall be extended to and capped at the finished surface level and installed at— (a) each point of connection; (b) even spacings not more than 30 m apart; (c) each end of any inclined jump-up that exceeds 6 m in length; (d) each connection to an existing site stormwater drain; and (e) at any change of direction greater than 45°." Note: "Inspection openings may be replaced by an inlet or stormwater pit." The 2003 edition says the same at clause 8.5.1 (PDF page 83). Read myself. The clause is worded for other than single dwellings; for a single dwelling it sets no spacing.
2. **Maximum run between openings or pits.** 30 m, even spacings (clause 7.4.1(b) above). Office figure: Not found (the digest records the office length as "Unknown", D-081).
3. **Bends in a run.** Clause 5.4.11.1 (PDF page 64): drains are to "(a) be laid with even gradients and straight runs and with a minimum number of changes of direction or change of cross-section; (b) be laid with any change of direction or cross-section occurring at either a fitting or at a pit". Read myself. No limit on the number of bends between pits: Not found.
4. **Allowed bend angles.** The standard gives no list of fitting angles for site stormwater drains. Angles that it does state: a branch or cut-in changes flow direction "between 45° and 90°" (Figure 7.7.1(C), PDF page 89); a graded jump-up has changes "not greater than 60° in the vertical plane" (clause 6.3.7(b)(i)); subsoil drains need a clean-out at each change of direction greater than 70° (clause 6.4.1(c)(ii)). Square junctions are allowed only at the top of a jump-up, as an inspection opening, or in place of a bend and opening at the top of a jump-up (clause 7.7.2). Read myself.
5. **Opening size and access.** Clause 7.4.2: DN150 or smaller, the same size as the drain; larger than DN150, not less than DN150. Clause 7.4.3: reached by a pit or a sealed riser to ground or floor level. Clause 7.5.1.1: pits are provided for junctions, changes of gradient and changes of direction. Read myself.
6. **Office rule, bends.** Guidebook v1.3 (PDF page 21, charged system step): "Avoid 90 degree bends and opt for 135 deg bends with Inspection Opening (IO) provision. Ensure that min 300mm head is available within charged system." Read myself.
7. **Office rule, other bends.** Kingston drainage guideline, mark-up list: "Pipe bends other than 45 deg to have inspection openings (IO)". Read myself. A 135 degree bend is a 45 degree change of direction (my reading), so this agrees with the standard's "greater than 45°", but item 6 asks for an IO even on a 135 degree bend.
8. **Office rule, where.** Guidebook v1.3 council RFI lists for Kingston, Glen Eira, Bayside, Monash and Frankston: "Inspection Openings (IOs) at change of direction". Design Checklist workbook: IOs "on either side" of a pipe under a garage. Guidebook element list item 18 (digest STAGE1_GUIDEBOOK_NOTES): "Inspection openings at changes of direction". Read myself, except the element list (digest).
9. **Near the building.** No office or standard wording on an IO "near the building" other than the garage rule in item 8 and the within-building access note in AS/NZS 3500.3:2003 (downpipe openings, clause 4.11(d)). Not found otherwise.

## Not found / could not read
1. Office maximum run between IOs or pits. Maximum number of bends between openings. A list of allowed fitting angles.
2. Council guidelines other than the five RFI lists: not searched line by line for bends (time).
3. The 2018 page numbers are the PDF page; printed page is two less.

## One recommendation
Use the standard as the rule and the office wording as the prompt: ask for an IO at any change of direction over 45 degrees, at connections, and at least every 30 m (other than single dwellings), and always for a 135 degree bend on a charged line and each side of a garage pipe. Warn on a 90 degree bend. Show the clause beside each prompt.
