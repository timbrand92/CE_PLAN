5. **Survey XREF detached after CDSURVEY approved it (BUG-1189, draft PR #1756):** with the fix, if the survey XREF is DETACHED after approval, CE stops offering the stored levels and prints "the XREF is no longer in this drawing". **Recommendation:** keep it as built, because CE cannot check a survey it can no longer see; typed levels still work and the switch can turn the check off. **Example on a job:** you approve the survey, then detach the heavy XREF to speed up the drawing; every later survey offer stops until you reattach it. **Other choice:** warn only and still offer the levels.

6. **Stage drawings: a BIND versus an accidental detach (BUG-1196 area, draft PR #1767):** CE cannot tell a deliberate BIND of a stage drawing from an accidental detach, so both now give a yellow warning, not a red QA Fail. **Recommendation:** keep it that way, because a false red Fail on a normal BIND would train drafters to ignore Fails. **Example on a job:** you bind the Stage 1 drawing into the master and CE warns once instead of failing the whole QA sheet. **Other choice:** treat any missing stage drawing as a Fail until the drafter confirms the BIND.
