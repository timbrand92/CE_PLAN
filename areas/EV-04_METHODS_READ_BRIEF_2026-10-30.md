## 1. What was tried, and why it failed

1. **Words on the footing plan PDF.** Footing plans carry a standard legend and notes that name piers, strip, pad, raft and stumps whether used or not. Across 991 structural jobs, most footing plans name 2 to 4 systems; only 59 name exactly one.
2. **Footing marks written on the PDF plan (P1, SF1, PF1, EB1, S1).** Short marks clash with section marks, panel marks and fabric names (S1, P1, F1). The test showed stumps on all 14 industrial jobs, which is wrong. Dropped.
3. **Conclusion:** the system actually used is drawn, not written. It has to be read from the drawing.

## 2. What to read (from the drawings, not the PDFs)

For each job's structural footing plan drawing (the copies method of Evidence Job 2, headless AutoCAD core console, never Tim's own AutoCAD session):

1. **Footing system:** which footing blocks are placed in model space and how many (pier, pad, stump, screw pile blocks); edge and internal beam lines on the beam layers (stiffened raft); waffle pod hatch or pod blocks (waffle raft); strip footing lines.
2. **Ground floor:** slab hatch or slab outline versus bearer and joist members on the ground floor framing.
3. **Walls and roof:** member schedule entries by member type (timber MGP / LVL, steel PFC / UB / SHS), and truss versus rafter members on the roof framing.
4. **Retention:** retaining wall or piled wall blocks and layers.
5. **Rule:** count only what is placed in the drawing, never what a legend or note lists. A block defined but not placed does not count.
