# Inputs and outputs of a civil job

Every input a civil design needs, grouped by where it comes from, and every output that lands on the drawing or goes beside it. Each says which sums (S-) and elements (E-) use it. Re-filed from the evidence on Mon 16 Nov 2026; not confirmed by the owner.

The item numbers here ("Input 1", "Output 1") are list numbers for this file only. They are not a register letter. They are never reused: new items go at the end of their part.

## How to add to this file

1. Add a new input at the END of Part A (next "Input" number) or a new output at the END of Part B (next "Output" number). Never renumber.
2. Say the group it belongs to (where it comes from, or where it lands), then "Used by:" with S- and E- numbers.
3. If who supplies an input is not known, write "Unknown" with the Q-number.
4. End every item with an "Evidence:" line. Update the "Next free" line at the end of the part.

## The five kinds of number (where every number comes from)

1. **From the council or permit:** method (given figures, Boyd's or OSD4W), storm events, coefficients, permitted discharge where given, minimum orifice, share of detention that must be underground, minimum grades, which tanks count.
2. **From outside sources:** rainfall for the site (Bureau), tank reuse litres and connected roof areas (STORM or sustainability report), survey levels, the discharge point and its invert (council, often assumed first).
3. **Measured off the drawing:** site area, roof and paving areas, pipe lengths, which pipe joins which pit.
4. **Chosen by the engineer:** pit positions, pipe sizes, inverts and grades, top water level, orifice size, which pipes and pits are detention.
5. **Worked out from the above:** flows, capacities, cover, depth to outlet, pit size, storage of each part, totals, pass or fail. This group is typed onto the sheet by hand today.

Evidence: ENG_NOTES "Where every number comes from".

## How often one fact is typed today (why "type once" matters)

1. On JOB_C the control pit's inlet invert appears 5 times (plan, schedule twice, detail twice); its outlet invert 3 times; its cover level 3 times; orifice size, head and top water level twice each; each tank's litres 3 to 4 times.
2. The same number lives in several places: spreadsheet, criteria box, pit schedule, plan text. The pipe table asks for each run's inverts, cover levels, length and size again.
3. Settled direction: type once, carried everywhere (D-002); disagreement may not stand silently (D-003).

Evidence: ELEMENT_LIST "Seen as a picture" item 7 and "First observations"; ENG_NOTES "Weak points" item 1; DECISIONS D-002, D-003.
