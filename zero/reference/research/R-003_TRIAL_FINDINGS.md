# R-003 Trial findings: what was tried in AutoCAD 2027, and what it changes in the build approach

**Made:** Mon 16 Nov 2026 (night), for the zero plan (Stage 4). **Source:** `stage4\TRIAL_RESULTS.md` (the full record, with the figures and the evidence file for every trial; this file summarises it and does not copy it). **Brief:** `stage4\TRIALS_BRIEF.md`.
**Builds on:** R-001 and R-002. R-002 said "nothing was tried in AutoCAD"; the trials below are the first things tried. They answer parts of R-002 sections A (notes and joins), B (events and overrules), D (Properties palette), E (live preview and Inspect) and F (ribbon tab and typed names).
**Status:** findings from trials, not decisions. A trial not run says NOT TESTED. Nothing from the trials was installed. (TRIAL_RESULTS "Where it stands")

## How the trials were run

1. **Where:** real AutoCAD 2027 under the owner's own profile, on made-up drawings, with his installed office plug-ins loaded in every full-AutoCAD run (they were not isolated, so any interaction with the trial plug-in is unmeasured). Trials 10 and 11 ran earlier, before the licence-incident limits. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 1 verdict, "What was not tested" item 10)
2. **Method that did not jam AutoCAD:** one plug-in command per test, a read-back after each, a check for stray windows before every send, never a long typed script. A 289-line typed script run with SCRIPT jammed the first attempt; the cause was not proven (the script, or the COPY repeat mode). About 60 steps then ran without a jam. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 1 item 1, "What must change" item 10)
3. **Limits that now bind any trial:** no AutoCAD profile made, copied, renamed, deleted or switched; no force-close; no licensing or sign-in; no registry or saved-setting change; one AutoCAD at a time and none while the owner's is open; leftovers untouched (D-116, D-120, D-121; `C:\TimOS\RULES.md` rule 1.9). Consequence: the headless engine cannot be used for trials without its isolate option (otherwise it loads the live office plug-in), so unattended headless runs are BLOCKED until the owner decides. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 10 item 6)

## Verdicts, one line each

1. **Trial 11, any direction ("hold what is fixed, the rest is worked out"):** PASS. Pure code, no AutoCAD. The mock-up figures were reproduced to the figure; a 20-pit branched network matched a separate hand calculation to 1 mm in 200 shuffled orders; a conflicting set gave one conflict of 60 mm naming only the held facts on its path; a set missing one fact said exactly one more must be held and listed the 16 of 77 that would do; loops were reported, not hung; 200 pits solved in a median 0.16 ms (slowest 2.1 ms). (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 11 items 2 to 4)
2. **Trial 10, headless predicts full:** PASS for scripted MOVE, COPY, MIRROR, ROTATE, ERASE, SCALE, UNDO and REDO on a pit block with joined pipes. All 123 event lines matched between the headless engine and full AutoCAD, step by step; the drawing ended identical (object handles shifted by one). Run under the old limits; cannot be repeated under the new ones. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 10)
3. **Trial 1, joins:** PASS for the commands tested; Xref attach and bind NOT TESTED. A soft pointer in the object's own extension dictionary stayed right through COPY and MIRROR; the end-of-command mover works (one undo); the close-step mover fails. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 1)
4. **Trial 3, no plugin:** PASS for sheet, plot, plain edit and report-back. Plots were identical pixel for pixel; AutoCAD LT and the owner's live drawing NOT TESTED. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 3)
5. **Trial 4, speed:** PASS for command and mover cost (about 40 ms to move one pit with its pipes on a 500-pit, 2500-object sheet). How a mouse drag with the ghost image feels was NOT measured. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 4)
6. **Trial 5, Properties palette:** BLOCKED for the real question (the palette is collapsed in the owner's profile and showing it changes a saved interface setting). At code level AutoCAD's own property list returned the plug-in's rows. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 5)
7. **Trial 6, window, live preview and Inspect:** PASS at 1920 x 1080; PARTIAL at 2560 x 1440 (simulated only); mouse pan and zoom during Inspect and the dark theme NOT TESTED. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 6)
8. **Trial 7, ribbon tab on selection, tips, typed names:** PARTIAL. Tab show and hide and the alias clash check work in code; how the tab and the tip look on screen, the rules-file route, workspace switch and restart NOT TESTED. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 7)
9. **Trial 9, update and rollback:** PASS for the file mechanics outside AutoCAD; the AutoCAD side (bundle folder, trust, load, lock) BLOCKED because it needs AutoCAD's own registrations in the registry. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 9)
10. **Trial 2, grips, and trial 8, double-click:** NOT TESTED; both need a real mouse (a Codex run or the owner). (TRIAL_RESULTS trials 2 and 8)

## Facts about how AutoCAD behaves (for lookup)

1. **Joins on copy:** after COPY or MIRROR of a pit with its pipes, a soft pointer and a hard pointer re-pointed to the new pit; a pit handle written in the note pointed back at the original (wrong, but detectable). A pipe copied alone stays pointed at the original pit. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 1 item 3.1, 3.2)
2. **Other commands:** MIRROR with originals erased, ROTATE, STRETCH, PASTECLIP, WBLOCK then INSERT then EXPLODE kept all three join forms right. STRETCH moved nothing unless the pit was on screen. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 1 item 3.4)
3. **Copy to another drawing (clipboard or WBLOCK style):** for pipes alone the pit was dragged in as an ownerless orphan; Autodesk's own warning about hard pointers (R-002 A item 8). Soft against hard could not be separated in this test. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 1 item 3.5)
4. **EXPLODE:** a pipe loses its extension dictionary (so its soft and hard pointers and its record) and keeps the note; a pit disappears and every pipe join reads ERASED (a clean break). (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 1 item 3.6)
5. **Save and reopen:** notes, records and joins stayed intact after BLOCK and a save and read-back in the current format, 2013 and 2000 formats, and after -PURGE of registered applications and AUDIT. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 1 item 3.7)
6. **A drawing-wide shelf keyed by object handle** was lost by COPY, PASTECLIP, WBLOCK/INSERT and a copy to another drawing: not usable for facts. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 1 item 3.8)
7. **Editing other objects from an object's own close step:** AutoCAD refused it ("object was open for write", and on undo "open for undo"). Doing it at the end of the command works and undoes as one step for MOVE and ROTATE. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 1 item 4)
8. **Events:** the transform overrule fires for MOVE and for the copies made by COPY and MIRROR. The object overrule's close call fires more often in full AutoCAD (83 against 65 over one run), so close counts are noise. The idle event fired 0 times in both. Window and crossing selection finds nothing in the headless engine, so scripted steps must select by object ID. A plug-in's own marker commands can eat an undo step unless flagged no-undo-marker. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 10 item 2)
9. **Plain AutoCAD with no plug-in:** the sheet looked and plotted identically; a pit moved with plain MOVE left its pipe ends where they were (nothing watching), so the join was left visibly broken; the position stamp in the extension dictionary survived save and reopen and let the plug-in report what changed on return. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 3 items 2 to 4)
10. **Speed on 2500 objects:** MOVE of one pit median 32.1 ms with no overrule, 32.3 ms with the filtered overrule, 40.4 ms with the mover on (3 pipe ends found by scanning every object). (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 4 item 2)
11. **Properties:** adding a provider to the polyline, block reference and base entity classes raised no error; AutoCAD's own machinery returned the plug-in's rows for a pipe (93 properties) and a pit (90) and none for a plain polyline (89); an editable row wrote back. Adding it once is enough (rows came twice from adding to a class and its parent). Whether the palette shows them is not seen. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 5 item 2)
12. **Window and preview:** a WPF window opened through AutoCAD's own modal call came out exactly 80% by 80% (1536 x 864 on a 1920 x 1080 screen). An off-screen snapshot preview of real drawing geometry redrew in a median 21 ms (1152 x 744) and 39 ms (1664 x 1032, simulated 2560 x 1440). Inspect through the supported start-user-interaction call hid the window, allowed a command-driven ZOOM, and returned it visible with the typed value kept; the toolkit's own "is visible" flag lies while hidden. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 6 items 2 to 4)
13. **Ribbon and aliases:** a tab added at run time showed for exactly one note-carrying pipe selected and hid otherwise, with no error and without taking the front. The owner's alias file (77 entries) has `C` as Copy; `CD`, `PIT`, `IO`, `RC` and `CP` did not clash. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 7 items 2 and 5)
14. **Update mechanics:** a file held open by a running program cannot be overwritten or deleted, so an update must go to a NEW folder; no administrator rights were needed; a versioned folder plus a one-line pointer file plus a hash check fell back from a broken 1.2 to 1.1 and rolled back to 1.0 by one pointer change. (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 9 item 3)
15. **Sums engine findings that need design choices:** whole-number grades are not unique (a grade worked from two inverts can be 1 in 59 where 1 in 60 was typed; both give the same fall; ruled in D-123); the sum a conflict is blamed on varies with where routes meet, so report "somewhere along P4 to P1" (ruled in D-125); when several standards could give way the last one filled absorbs the whole difference (ruled in D-124: nothing bends by itself). (TRIAL_RESULTS trial 11 item 5)

## What changes in the build approach

1. **Joins:** store each join as a soft pointer in the object's own extension dictionary, with identity and short facts in the note; check every join on open and say plainly which are broken (erased pit, pipe copied alone, exploded pit). Do not use the drawing shelf for facts. (TRIAL_RESULTS "What must change" items 1, 2)
2. **Moving joined pipe ends:** at the end of the command, never in the object's close step, so a move is one undo. (TRIAL_RESULTS "What must change" item 3)
3. **Position stamp paired with the join check:** plain AutoCAD can move a pit and leave its pipes, so the stamp says what moved and the join check says what no longer meets. (TRIAL_RESULTS "What must change" item 4)
4. **Sums engine:** plain code with no AutoCAD in it, far faster than needed; the foundation block "sum" stands. (TRIAL_RESULTS "What must change" item 5)
5. **Preview:** a real AutoCAD picture of real objects, so no second drawing path; Inspect by the supported call. (TRIAL_RESULTS "What must change" item 6)
6. **Properties:** do not promise "facts in AutoCAD's own Properties palette" until the palette is seen showing the rows. Fallbacks (our own side palette, or a small C++ helper for the palette alone) are not built and need the owner's ruling. (TRIAL_RESULTS "What must change" item 7; trial 5 item 6)
7. **Typed names:** give `C` a different short name or ask the owner (ruled: D-054). (TRIAL_RESULTS "What must change" item 8)
8. **Headless:** do not rely on it for anything about selection, idle or the screen. (TRIAL_RESULTS "What must change" item 9)
9. **Nothing in the trials made the approach fail:** ordinary objects with hidden notes held for looks, plotting, plain editing, copy, mirror, rotate, stretch, save and reopen, with the exceptions above. (TRIAL_RESULTS "What must change" item 11)
