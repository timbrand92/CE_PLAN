## TWL and outlet review in drawing terms

Candidate list is evidence, not a sorted “highest wins” decision engine. A higher TG connected to the selected system may be relevant; an unrelated higher item is not automatically controlling. Confirmed topology and engineering intent determine candidate applicability. If that cannot be established, display it as unresolved rather than quietly choose another candidate.

Show floor levels and other safety/context levels as their own roles. Do not relabel them TWL. Unknown datum, datum mismatch or uncertain level interpretation prevents a supported comparison; no zero-reference substitution. A typed level records value, units/datum, reason and reviewed evidence, while preserving the candidate list.

For an outlet, keep permissible/nominated discharge, calculated discharge and adopted physical opening distinct. Display the relevant head/reference basis supplied by the supported method, not a new formula assumed by this plan. Calculated opening, commercially selected/adopted opening and resulting discharge require an explicit comparison. Parent coefficient and rounding choices remain unresolved; CE cannot silently round upward or select an office coefficient to make the sequence look complete.

## Required volume versus physical provision

The comparison names exactly what was counted. An excluded pipe stays excluded; a duplicate system claim is a finding, not extra capacity. A missing or changed contribution cannot be silently retained as current merely because its previous numeric value remains visible.

Reuse capacity, detention credit and physical capacity are separate where another owner supplies tank data. This record only consumes the explicitly confirmed applicable contribution; it does not determine a tank's credit, size, manufacturer detail or internal outlet. Water Tank remains last and separately owned.

“Provided exceeds required” is a bounded volume comparison, not blanket compliance or proof of outlet control, hydraulic performance, freeboard, treatment, access or construction suitability. Any displayed pass/fail must name its actual checked requirement and established authority; unknown required criteria remain Unknown.

## Working a revision during design

Changing only the candidate source while retaining governing pins raises suitability review. Confirming a different governing TWL/discharge/area/member basis makes actual dependent calculations and outputs out of date. Draft calculations remain separately labelled from the last confirmed result, following root25/26.

Review a proposed drawing change before execution, then deliberately refresh the physical comparison. A calculated size does not prove that the changed asset now exists in the drawing. Manual exclusions, required volume and unrelated systems remain intact. A failed or partial drawing change follows its owner receipt/readback, not an automatic calculation retry.
