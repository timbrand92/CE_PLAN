# Civil drawings — CE practice (DRAFT, 26 Sep 2026)

Read-only study approved by Tim: 9 civil sets (8 jobs, 2024-2026), all small residential/townhouse jobs (no SPS,
raingarden or GIO in the sample). Anonymised (DATA-01). Items 1, 2, 4 callout, 7 (layout and remarks), 8 and 9 titling CONFIRMED by Tim, 26 Sep 2026 (decisions file).

1. **Pits:** P1, P2...; existing council pits EP / SEP; boundary junction pits JP1; size only in the schedule.
2. **Pipes:** "100 DIA. UPVC PIPE AT 1 IN 100" (Tim 26 Sep: no MIN./MAX. suffix, BUG-027 stands, although older drawings show it); "225 DIA. RCP / RRJ PIPE AT 1 IN 200 - 21.5m DETENTION"; IL text
   "IL 33.00" with a short leader; inlet/outlet ILs stacked either side of a pit.
3. **Sources:** DP downpipes; IO inspection openings; OF overflows (no FO or GIO seen); grated pits and trench drains via
   legend ("100/200 WIDE TRENCH WITH CLASS C GRATE").
4. **Tanks:** WT, WT1...; callout with capacity, make, dimensions and the detention/reuse split; riser/dropper notes.
5. **LPD:** "CONNECTION TO LPD NOTE:" - connect to council pit at approx. IL to council satisfaction, plus bullets.
6. **Existing services:** "Ex. SEWER DRAIN" etc., single-letter line codes (S, W, T).
7. **Pit Schedule:** "STORMWATER PIT SCHEDULE". Column order confirmed from the rendered C01 of the most recent issue
   (26 Sep): PIT No. | PIT SIZE | INLET (FROM, DIA, INVERT LEVEL) | OUTLET (DIA, INVERT LEVEL) | COVER LEVEL | DEPTH
   TO OUTLET | TYPE OF LID AND REMARKS. One row per inlet: the first inlet shares the pit's row, each further inlet
   gets its own row with only FROM/DIA/INVERT filled. FROM uses the source code (PP, TG ...). A double pit lists
   each size on its own line in PIT SIZE and joins lids with "+" ("Class A Solid Lid + Class A Grated Lid"). Levels
   to 2 decimals; three standard remarks under every schedule.
8. **C01 notes:** blocks GENERAL, CIVIL WORKS, STORMWATER DRAINAGE, SITE PREPARATION (standard boilerplate);
   "ON SITE DETENTION CRITERIA" block (PSD, head, orifice dia, TWL, volume required per storage element, total);
   council blocks for some councils (e.g. a full City of Kingston notes block), otherwise the LPD note.
9. **C02:** either "OUTFALL DRAIN PLAN AND DETAILS" or "STORMWATER DRAINAGE DETAIL SHEET"; recurring details (junction
   pit, orifice pit, water tank, pipe bedding, services penetration, charged system, sawcut, lap splice, re-entrant
   corner); titled by description ("ORIFICE PIT P1 DETAIL"), no detail numbers seen.
10. **Legend:** "CIVIL WORKS LEGEND" with a long fixed list (FFL, IL, CL, JP, grated pit, DP, trench drain, AG, kerb,
    IO, existing services, TPZ, PP, charged pipe, suspended pipe, overland flowpath...).
11. **Levels and grades:** plain 2-decimal levels, "RL" for spot levels, AHD stated in notes; IL / CL / TWL prefixes;
    grades written "1 IN 100", never percent.
