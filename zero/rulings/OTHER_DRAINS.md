# Current rulings: Other drains (AG, perimeter, strip, charged, suspended, trench grate, spoon)

Current rulings as of 20 Nov 2026 (Melbourne), from decisions D-001 to D-383 (D-298 to D-383 filed 20 Nov 2026). If this page and DECISIONS.md disagree, DECISIONS.md wins and this page is stale.

## What is decided now

1. **In the first release:** trench grate, AG and perimeter drains (D-223). **Paused by D-337 and D-338 (19 Nov):** not in the one-job release; listed in P-09.
2. **Strip drain is parked:** offered and not ticked (D-223).
3. **AG drains have no invert levels** (D-040).
4. **All pipes act the same or similar way:** each part sits in the same spot on every pipe kind and slides only to clear a clash (D-041).
5. **Charged and suspended pipes are kinds of the Pipe tool** in the first release (THE_PLAN section 3, from D-097 to D-110). **Not in Stage 1 (D-377 item 1, 20 Nov):** "yes ordinary for now because theyre all the same visually so we will do the rest later, there is also the rising main for later"; charged, suspended, detention, twin and the rising main later (Tim's room to confirm charged is out).
6. **A charged pipe that runs from a downpipe to a water tank only** is a situation the design must cover (D-038).
7. **Bends on every kind of pipe** (D-189).
8. **Wide, along-a-line window style** for pipe of every kind, AG drain, trench grate, spoon drain and strip drain (D-067; the planning chat's sorting, Tim may correct). **Superseded by D-296 (18 Nov):** the preview sits at the left in every window, so the wide style no longer decides where it sits (item 12).

### Added 18 Nov 2026, D-230 to D-297

9. **The trench grate keeps TG (D-233):** "Keep TG; it takes over when the old plugin goes". Until the old plug-in is removed the new tool is tested under its full word TRENCHGRATE (TG is the old plug-in's trench grate command today).
10. **The strip drain label and the trench grate block are out of the Blocks palette (D-232):** Tim: "covered in the plugin"; they stay in the master drawing as spares.
11. **Label words and the cover check by pipe kind (D-268, D-269):** charged, suspended and detention each have an office wording (PIPE item 35); a charged line gets the ordinary cover check, a suspended pipe shows "no cover check: suspended" in grey.
12. **The preview of every window, these included, sits at the left (D-296);** its size and form are OPEN under D-297. **Pipe changed to the top strip by D-313 (19 Nov).**

### Added 20 Nov 2026, D-298 to D-383

13. **Paused (D-337, D-338):** AG, perimeter and trench grate are not in the one-job release.
14. **Ordinary pipe only in Stage 1 (D-377 item 1);** the rising main is named as a later kind. Every kind is a data row from the start (Tim's room's recommendation with TR-026: "keep it simple but record everything so ... everything is scalable and can be built upon").

## Changed along the way

1. **FUNCTION_MAP FM-017 recommended building AG and perimeter as a kind of Pipe with no inverts, and FM-016 recommended parking strip drain; D-223 (18 Nov) put AG and perimeter in and parked strip.** How AG and perimeter are built (kind of Pipe or own tool) is still open.
2. **Other drains in the first release (D-223) and charged and suspended pipes (THE_PLAN section 3) were paused or moved later by D-337, D-338 and D-377 (19 and 20 Nov).**
