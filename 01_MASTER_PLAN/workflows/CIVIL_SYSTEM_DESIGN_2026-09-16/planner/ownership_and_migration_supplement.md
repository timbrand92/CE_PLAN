## Repair and migration are different doors

| Door | Required behaviour | Status and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary Repair | Start with a read-only scan; show target, reason, protected-content limit and a preview; the user chooses a permitted repair. Cancel/Escape makes no change; an accepted drawing repair is one Undo and keeps a history. | **Proposed general workflow.** It preserves native locked/Xref/REFEDIT/foreign-content protections. |
| Approved pipe-upgrade exception | A pipe upgrade may occur on **existing-job open and each pipe open/edit** only after the approved compatibility/readers and 2024-enabler gates pass. It makes one first backup, one AutoCAD Undo and one visible count/backup line. If the gates fail, it must not silently perform a blind upgrade. | **Tim-approved, scoped migration exception** in Test Day §§6j–6n. It is not permission to make every Repair Center action automatic. |
