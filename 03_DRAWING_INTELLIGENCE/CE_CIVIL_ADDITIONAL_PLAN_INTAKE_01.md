# CE Civil — additional plan intake

**Purpose:** add new real-project evidence without repeating the existing 30-project review.

## What Tim can send

For each added project, one line in chat is enough:

```text
Project folder:
Why this job is useful or difficult:
Known Civil drawing/problem (if any):
Project state: Fresh / Partly complete / Issued-complete / Under revision / Unknown
Was CE ACAD used? Yes / No / Partly / Unknown
```

Tim does not need to prepare or rename files. Codex will locate the relevant Civil drawings, architecture, survey, calculations and authority information read-only.

## Best next evidence — in priority order

1. Civil and Structural shown together, especially pits/pipes near footings, beams, columns, slab thickenings or set-downs.
2. A multi-level job where one logical pipe continues across basement, ground and roof plans.
3. A pumped or charged system with a proven source, rising main and discharge point.
4. A job with several legal discharge points or separate drainage branches.
5. A difficult council approval/revision history where the final authority-approved set can be identified exactly.
6. A CE ACAD job where normal drafting was blocked, cancelled, slowed or reclassified incorrectly.
7. Older plain AutoCAD Civil drawings containing useful office-standard text, arrows, schedules or details.
8. A matched set showing the same project fresh, partly complete, issued and then revised, where available.

## What Codex will return for each project

- what is directly proven by the DWG/DXF, PDF, calculation or authority record;
- the Civil network and drawing-stage relationships that are clear, uncertain or conflicting;
- the drafting rule demonstrated by the plan, schedule, long section, notes and details;
- the exact CE ACAD behaviour helped, contradicted or missing;
- the smallest AutoCAD regression example;
- whether the finding is office-wide, council-specific, client-specific or project-specific; and
- any question needing a draftsperson or engineer, kept in the appropriate question group.

## Safety

- Source project files remain read-only.
- Unknown remains Unknown.
- A drawing convention does not become an engineering rule without approval.
- New evidence may challenge the model and current plugin; it is not required to agree with either.
