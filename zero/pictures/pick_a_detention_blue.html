Known (from the office files)

The ordinary new pipe is on STORMWATER PIPE, colour 150, DASHED. The plot style prints 150 in its own blue, 0.25 mm, 100%. Colours 140, 152 and 160 have no row in the plot style table, so they take "all others: own colour, 100%". None of the three is used on any office layer. Rulings D-083 and D-118: own kind, own layer, a slightly different blue, picked from a sheet.

Assumed (mine, please check)

The screen colours (0, 64, 255 and so on) are AutoCAD's standard colour table, which is not in the office files. I assumed paper prints the same blue the screen shows. The dark screen, the plan piece and both legend wordings are made up for this picture.
