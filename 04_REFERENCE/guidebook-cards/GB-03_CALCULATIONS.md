2. **Computations sheet. Modify.** Page 1 gets the OSD4W-style inputs block (existing and proposed areas and coefficients, Tc, Tso, storm frequencies); the logo and job strip repeat on every page; no "Note:" lines; one orifice block with diameter, plate height, head to the orifice centre, flow against permitted discharge; pipe-by-pipe and pit-by-pit tables. Every figure agrees with the drawing's criteria box.
3. **Pump sheet. Modify.** Keep the storm sweep; print the book's fixed 2-hour 1-in-10 answer beside it (30 minutes pumped, 1 per cent of catchment term, wet well). In-ground 1 in 10, basement 1 in 100. Pump flow not above the permitted discharge. The Bayside 1 in 20 rule goes unless someone points to its source. The 3 m3 figure stays a review flag (R-14).
4. **Soakage sheet. Modify.** Add the soil factor (sand 0.5, sandy clay 1, clay 2), set-backs from structures (sand 1, sandy clay 2, rock 2, medium clay 4, heavy clay 5 m), the 1-in-100 storm sweep, minimum soil rate 100 mm/h for Kingston, Frankston and Mornington. Warn on set-back and low soil rate.
