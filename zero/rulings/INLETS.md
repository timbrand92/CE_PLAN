# Current rulings: Inlets (downpipe, overflow, FO, inspection opening, GIO, floor waste, trench grate)

Current rulings as of 18 Nov 2026, 1:40 pm (Melbourne), from decisions D-001 to D-229. If this page and DECISIONS.md disagree, DECISIONS.md wins and this page is stale.

## What is decided now

### Which inlets, and their names

1. **In the first release:** "Downpipe and the other inlets" (D-097), and trench grate (D-223).
2. **Typed names:** DOWNPIPE / DP, OVERFLOW / OF, FO / FO, INSPECTION / IO, GIO / GIO, FLOORWASTE / FW (D-101); each still to be proven free in AutoCAD 2027.
3. **FO has no longer name:** "Leave it as FO only" (D-059).
4. **IO keeps its name** although it sits on AutoCAD's own IO short name: "Keep IO" (D-054).

### Placing and moving

5. **An inlet such as a GIO goes exactly where the drafter clicks along the pipe,** slides along the pipe by its grip afterwards, and can also be placed off a pipe (D-050; the last two parts are the planning chat's answer given to Tim the same day).
6. **Placing many, for example 12 downpipes:** one window, then pick after pick, each numbered in turn, Enter to finish (D-186).
7. **An inspection opening is offered, never forced,** at a branch that joins a bend in the main pipe on a long run with no inspection opening (D-081).

### The Inlet window

8. **Square preview style** for a thing at one spot (D-067; the planning chat's sorting put downpipe, overflow, FO, IO, GIO and floor waste here, Tim may correct).
9. **"Tick: right track, same as the others"** and "yep happy witht hat window"; not a final acceptance, and symbols are to be the office's own blocks when built (D-111, D-112).
10. **On the bench the Inlet window looks good** (D-169).
11. **The inlet symbols are "The WITH MASK set":** the dynamic masked downpipe, IO, GIO, overflow, grated overflow and floor waste, from above and to below; the older floor waste blocks leave the palette (D-229).
12. **The inlet does not know where the house wall is;** the wall on the bench is made-up scenery (D-162, answer by the planning chat to Tim's question).

## Changed along the way

1. **TRENCHGRATE / TG as an inlet was only a recommendation (FUNCTION_MAP FM-032); D-223 (18 Nov) put trench grate in the first release.** Whether it is an inlet or a kind of pipe, and its typed name, are still open (D-067 sorted trench grate into the wide, along-a-line style).
