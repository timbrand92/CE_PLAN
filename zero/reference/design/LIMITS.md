# Rules and limits register (L-)

Every rule or limit the office designs to, one item each, with where each value comes from. Re-filed from the evidence on Mon 16 Nov 2026. Nothing here is proposed as the office rule. Where values differ, both are recorded and the owner rules (D-003, D-022, Q-004).

## How to add to this file

1. Take the number on the last line ("Next free number"). Never reuse or renumber.
2. Copy the eight headings below in the same order. Give every value its own source (council name, AS/NZS 3500.3, guidebook, workbook name, sheet note).
3. If two values differ, list both under "Conflict" and do not choose. Only a D-number settles it ("Settled by D-0xx").
4. Under "Order of authority" say which listed source ranks first by D-022 (council, then AS/NZS 3500.3, then the office guidebook).
5. Finish with an "Evidence:" line and move "Next free number" on by one. A council-only value also goes in that council's file in `reference\councils\`.

## L-001 Minimum pipe grade

1. **Rule:** A gravity pipe is laid no flatter than a minimum grade. Detention pipes may be flatter.
2. **Value or values:** Gravity pipes 1 in 100. Detention pipes: 1 in 200 for 225 and larger (some councils); or 225 at 1 in 200, 300 at 1 in 250, larger at 1 in 300. Seen on jobs: 225 at 1 in 150, 1 in 190 and 1 in 200.
3. **Applies to:** Every gravity pipe (E-001); detention pipes (E-004).
4. **Source of each value:** 1 in 100: guidebook; sheet general notes. 1 in 200 for 225 and larger: guidebook ("some councils allow"). 225 / 300 / larger at 1 in 200 / 250 / 300: design checklist workbook. 1 in 150: CIV-02. 1 in 190: CIV-11. 1 in 200: JOB_B, CIV-05 outfall.
5. **Conflict:** Detention pipe grade: guidebook (1 in 200 for 225 and larger) against the design checklist (a stepped list by size).
6. **Order of authority per D-022:** Council first where it states a grade; no AS/NZS 3500.3 value is quoted in the evidence; then guidebook.
7. **Status:** Gravity 1 in 100 not in conflict. Detention grades need the owner's ruling (same kind of ruling as Q-004; not listed there by name).

Evidence: STUDY_B section 3 item 1; GUIDEBOOK_NOTES "Rules of thumb" item 1.

## L-015 Pumps

1. **Rule:** A pumped system follows AS/NZS 3500.3 section 8, with office practice on top.
2. **Value or values:**
   1. Duplicate pumps.
   2. Storage pumped in 30 minutes plus wet well at least the 1 in 10, 2 hour runoff.
   3. Wet well at least 1% of catchment area and never under 3 cubic metres.
   4. High alarm no higher than 100 above the inlet invert.
   5. Inlet invert at least 100 above top water level.
   6. Ladder if deeper than 1.2 m.
   7. Office practice: pump rate = permitted discharge; basements with habitable risk for 1 in 100; well impervious, no groundwater.
   8. One council "discourages" pumps and wants prior approval; one requires gravity at the kerb.
   9. "Small pumps" tab: wet well 1.5 to 3 by a rule of thumb.
3. **Applies to:** Pump station and rising main (E-059); S-014, S-015; K-007, K-012.
4. **Source of each value:** (1) to (6): AS/NZS 3500.3 section 8 as copied in the office pump workbook. (7): office practice (workbook, job sums). (8): Glen Eira ("kerb and channel connection must be gravity fed"); Darebin ("gravity only, no pumps" on CIV-05); the council that "discourages" is not named. (9): pump workbook "small pumps" tab.
5. **Conflict:** The "small pumps" tab allows a wet well below the standard's 3 cubic metres. Workbook versions differ on static rise and bottom level (S-015).
6. **Order of authority per D-022:** Council first (gravity-only rules); then AS/NZS 3500.3 section 8; the "small pumps" rule of thumb is an office document below the standard.
7. **Status:** Needs the owner's ruling Q-003.

Evidence: STUDY_B section 3 item 15, section 1g items 1, 4 and 5, 1e item 1, section 5 item 6.

## L-016 Soakage

1. **Rule:** Soakage is only used on suitable soil, with set distances and a silt trap, and sized with a moderating factor.
2. **Value or values:** Soil rate at least 100 mm/h; groundwater information; overflow for the 1 in 100; modules 1 m from footings, 2 m from boundaries, 1.5 m from tree zones where possible; silt trap with mesh before the modules; at the low point, not in a blocked-off area. Moderating factor 0.5 for sand, 1.0 for sandy clay. Void share 0.95.
3. **Applies to:** Soakage pits and silt pits (E-060); S-016, S-017; K-008.
4. **Source of each value:** Distances, soil rate, silt trap, low point: design checklist workbook and guidebook. Moderating factor: job sums, from the Melbourne Water procedure. Void share: soakage workbook.
5. **Conflict:** Storm to size for: 1 in 10 (CIV-08 Rev A) against 1 in 100 (Rev B and C, after the council asked).
6. **Order of authority per D-022:** Council (and the Melbourne Water chapter it points to) first; then guidebook and checklist.
7. **Status:** Whether 1 in 100 with the moderating factor is the starting point for every council: Unknown (Q-013).

Evidence: STUDY_B section 3 item 16, section 1h items 3 to 5.
