**First start and help**

1. **Splash at start-up: how long?** Rec: at least 1 second, gone by 3, no click. Example: CE logo shows while AutoCAD finishes loading,
   then disappears by itself.
2. **Very first start: show the welcome card instead of What's New?** Rec: yes, only the welcome card that first time. Example: a new
   colleague sees one card "Draw a pipe, place a pit, run the checks", not two windows.
3. **You said "No" to a practice drawing on 26 Sep, then "Yes" on 27 Sep. Which?** Rec: yes, a made-up house job (not a real client
   job) in Help, a fresh copy each time. Example: Help > Open practice job, draw on it, wreck it, open it again clean.
4. **You said tips show 3 times (26 Sep), then once (27 Sep). Which?** Rec: once, changeable in Settings. Example: the first PITSCHEDULE
   shows "Tip: click a row to zoom to that pit", then never again.
5. **"Send to admin" saves the report to the office feedback folder; should CE also email it?** Rec: no email; it lands in your Manager as
   a count. Example: Max sends a problem, your Manager shows "1 report waiting".
6. **Recent jobs: which drawing opens when you click a job?** Rec: the one you had open last in that job. Example: click 25-123 and your
   C01 stormwater plan opens, because that is what you were last in.
7. **Shorten the start-up command-line message to one line?** Rec: yes, "CE ready. New? Click Start Here on the CE tab." Example: today it
   lists CD, PIT, PITSCHEDULE, CDEDIT, CDHELP and CDREPAIR to someone on their first day.
8. **The "Civil" button on the CE panel should change to "Structural" when you switch to a structural drawing?** Rec: yes now; the
   middle panels swapping comes later with the ribbon work. Example: open S03, the button reads "Structural".

**Framing plans, roof down**

9. **"P" on a framing plan: timber post or purlin?** Rec: P = timber post (your 26 Sep words); purlins get another letter you choose.
   Example: today the rafter tool tags a purlin P1 and the post tool also uses P.
10. **Member schedule, first time on a job: on the framing sheet or a separate schedule sheet?** Rec: on the framing sheet; CE then
    remembers and offers the same spot on the next plan. Example: roof plan's schedule bottom-right of S07, first floor's goes
    bottom-right of S05.
11. **Beam hover line status words OK?** Rec: "calc approved", "calc done, not approved", "no calc yet", "Unknown". Example: rest the
    cursor on FB3: "FB3 · 250 x 90 PFC · 3.6 m · no calc yet".
12. **Tie-down types TD1 strap, TD2 bolted, TD3 proprietary: real names?** Rec: keep them as placeholders until you name the office's
    usual three. Example: the prompt would read "Tie-down type [TD1 strap/TD2 bolted/TD3 proprietary]".
13. **One switch for all the roof-down help (markers from above, carried posts, footing proposals), trial on your PC for a week?** Rec:
    yes. Example: turn on once; open the first floor and see grey "post from roof" markers.
14. **Beam layers: you typed "beams" and "beams lower"; every CE drawing today uses BEAM and BEAM LOWER (no S). Keep without the S?** Rec:
    keep BEAM / BEAM LOWER so old and new drawings match. Example: beams drawn with "1" go on BEAM, with "2" on BEAM LOWER.
15. **May CE count, on past jobs (local only, numbers only), how many markups, re-issues and members each framing plan had, to learn the
    best layouts?** Rec: yes, later, after you see the first ten counts. Example: "Job 24-0xx roof: 3 markups, 0 re-issues, 1.2 members per
    10 m2".
