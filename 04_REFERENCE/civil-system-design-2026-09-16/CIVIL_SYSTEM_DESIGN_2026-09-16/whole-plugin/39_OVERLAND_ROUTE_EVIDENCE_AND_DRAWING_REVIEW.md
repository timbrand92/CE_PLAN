## Fictional beginning-of-job example

A source report requires overflow consideration. The plan has a selected grate, a potential path beside a building, one uncertain doorway threshold and a road-edge destination with unconfirmed receiving conditions.

## During design: retain separate roles and identities

- A reported flood level is not a survey ground level. Show its event/scenario, location, datum, source and applicability where provided; never substitute it into the ground surface silently.
- A nominated overland route is not the pipe network. The two may have a confirmed overflow relationship, but matching plan crossings do not create it.
- A boundary is not proof of a flow corridor width; known obstructions/openings and usable extent need their own reviewed evidence.
- A pump/ramp flow figure from another calculation is an input/result with a stated scope. It does not by itself verify this route's capacity or safety.
- A tree zone, retaining wall or slab edge remains its own sourced object/constraint. Highlight potential conflict for review; do not clip/move/delete constraints to fit a preferred route.

Selecting or inspecting protected Xref/locked/foreign evidence never permits write-through, detaching or cloning around protection. Any local permanent annotation is a distinct reviewed drawing action and cannot claim ownership of the source geometry.

## Revision, cancellation and output

New report evidence prompts suitability review while existing governing pins stay visible. An adopted relevant level, geometry, opening or receiving condition change invalidates the actual dependent assessment/output; do not invalidate unrelated routes merely because a document was added. A moved manual arrow changes presentation, not the governing hydraulic assessment, unless it explicitly changes the reviewed route identity/geometry.

If an actual route geometry change is proposed, compare old/current/pending route and affected constraints before accepting. Preserve manual annotation positions unless that exact presentation is being reviewed. Drawing changes while a placement preview is open invalidate affected placement, even where engineering numbers are unchanged.

Cancel a review clears its temporary overlays and keeps the confirmed route/evidence. A prior accepted line/annotation change remains under its actual owner's Undo/save boundary. Save/reopen must retain source/datum/route identity and findings; arrow visibility alone is not proof the review record persisted.

Issue preparation shows unresolved overland evidence as a scoped finding and records Tim/engineer's disposition under the existing QA/issue policy. This record does not invent a universal issue refusal or waive a required assessment. Source/assessment history and actual output remain distinguishable.
