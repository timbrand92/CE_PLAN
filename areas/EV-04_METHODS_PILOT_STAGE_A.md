# EV-04 construction methods pilot: footing systems, 78 jobs (30 Oct 2026)

**Brief:** `areas\EV-04_METHODS_READ_BRIEF_2026-10-30.md`. **Status:** pilot done, **stopped for Tim's check.** Counts only; per-job detail local in `C:\ce_evidence_local\ev04_library\`.

## 1. How it was read

1. **From the drawings, not the PDFs.** Evidence Job 2 already scanned these jobs' structural drawings with the headless AutoCAD reader and kept how many objects sit on each layer and how many of each block are placed. No new AutoCAD run was needed.
2. **Used versus legend:** a system counts as used when its layer or block carries 10 or more objects (30 or more for waffle pods). 1 to 9 objects is a standard detail or legend only. On these jobs the two groups sit clearly apart for piers, screw piles and waffle pods (used jobs carry 14 to 1,500 objects).
3. **Pad footings and slab on ground could not be read:** the CE drawings have no layer or block that is only for them.
4. **70 of 78 jobs had a structural drawing read.** The other 8 are civil only or had no structural drawing in the scan.

## 2. Proposed mapping (Tim to confirm or correct)

1. **Bored piers:** layer "BORED PIER UNDER" or "BORED PIER", block "BORED PIER ONLY"; used at 10 or more objects.
2. **Screw piles:** layer "SCREW PILE", block "SCREW PILE" or "SCREW PILES"; used at 10 or more objects.
3. **Stumps:** layer "STUMPS"; used at 10 or more objects.
4. **Strip footings:** layer "STRIP FOOTINGS"; used at 10 or more objects.
5. **Waffle raft:** layer "WAFFLE PODS"; used at 30 or more objects.
6. **Stiffened raft (internal beams):** layer "SLAB INTERNAL BEAM"; used at 10 or more objects.

## 4. Doubts

1. **Stiffened raft may be high on small jobs** (4 of 5 small elements). Standard beam details are drawn on the same "SLAB INTERNAL BEAM" layer, and beam linework is less clear-cut than pier blocks. A higher cut-off or "beam lines on the footing plan only" would fix it; needs Tim's view.
2. **"None found" is not "no footings".** It means none of the six mapped layers or blocks carries 10 or more objects: pad footings, other layer names, or footings drawn in an xref.
3. **Not checked against a single real drawing.** These are counts from saved scan data only.
