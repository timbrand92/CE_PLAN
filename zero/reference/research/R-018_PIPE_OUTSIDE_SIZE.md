# R-018 The outside size of each pipe, for the cover check

**Written:** Fri 20 Nov 2026, evening (PC clock), by Tim's room. **Feeds:** U-086 (the pipe's outside size for cover to the top of the pipe); root cause C9.

**What was checked and what was not.** Checked at source by Tim's room: the office Stormpro cover spreadsheet, which holds mean outside / inside diameters 169 / 148 (150), 259 / 226 (225), 343 / 300 (300), 428 / 374 (375). Everything else below was read by a research helper on the Team account and has NOT been re-read by the room, including the clause wording of the plumbing standard. Sources are named in words; no web addresses. Not yet put to Tim.

## The question
What is the outside diameter (or wall) of each pipe, so cover can be measured to the top of the pipe, and how do the office and the standard say cover is measured?

## What was found
1. **Stormpro (office guidebook v1.3, page 18, Figure 9, a manufacturer extract; I viewed the page image myself).** Mean outside / inside diameter in mm: DN150 169 / 148; DN225 259 / 226; DN300 343 / 300; DN375 428 / 374. Inner wall thickness: 1.2, 1.6, 2.0, 2.4. The office spreadsheet "pipe cover stormpro.xlsx" (read myself) holds the same OD and ID figures.
2. **How the office Stormpro cover checker works (my arithmetic on the spreadsheet).** It subtracts (OD + ID) / 2 from the ground-to-invert depth, which equals the invert plus the inside diameter plus one wall: the top of the pipe. Minimum depth it shows at 300 cover: 150 is 458.5; 225 is 542.5; 300 is 621.5; 375 is 701 (mm).
3. **UPVC sewer pipe (Iplex "PVC-U DWV Sewer Pipe, Non-Pressure, Pipeline Dimensions", V1 2019, pages 1 and 2; read myself).** Minimum mean outside diameter: DN100 110; DN150 160; DN225 250; DN300 315; DN375 400. Minimum wall: DN100 3.0 (SN6) or 3.9 (SN10); DN150 SN8 5.2 (solvent weld) or 4.7 (rubber ring); DN225 SN8 8.2 or 7.2; DN300 SN8 10.4 or 9.1; DN375 SN8 11.1 (rubber ring).
4. **Same outside diameters in the standard.** AS/NZS 1260:2009 (office PDF), Table 4.1, page 17: minimum mean outside diameter 110.0, 160.0, 250.0, 315.0, 400.0 for DN100, 150, 225, 300, 375. Read myself, in text order, so the column layout is probable, not certain.
4a. **DN90:** no UPVC size is listed at 90 in either document. Not found.
5. **Concrete pipe outside diameter (Humes Concrete Pipe Manual, Table 4.2, printed page 17; I viewed the page image myself).** Standard-strength rows in bold: DN225 279; DN300 362; DN375 445. Other rows of the same table give larger values (for example 305, 381, 457), so the figure depends on the class and ID chosen. Rocla "Pipe Product Details" (flush joint, "indicative") gives 279, 362, 445. DN100 and DN150 concrete: Not found.
6. **How cover is measured, standard.** AS/NZS 3500.3:2018 (office PDF), Table 6.2.5, title: "MINIMUM PIPE COVER—FINISHED SURFACE TO TOP OF PIPE"; footnote: "Below the underside of the pavement." The office guidebook reproduces the table as Figure 10 (page 19). Read myself. The 2003 edition table says the same in digest R-013 (second-hand).
7. **How cover is measured, office.** Guidebook page 18: the Stormpro table is "useful in initial pit and pipe sizing and ensuring adequate pipe cover"; page 48: "Minimum pit depths with the pipe cover of 300mm considering STORMPRO PIPE". Digest LIMITS.md L-005 (second-hand): minimum pit depth is inside diameter plus wall plus 300.
8. **Under pavement.** Clause 6.2.5 pavement figures run from the underside of the pavement, not the finished surface (R-013, which also records that one council measures its 300 to the underside of the driveway; second-hand).
9. **The word "crown"** does not appear in the 2018 standard's text. Not found.

## Not found / could not read
1. DN90 outside diameter (any material). DN100 and DN150 concrete.
2. Pipe bedding and trench details on issued sheets (drawings not in the permitted folders). One council trench drawing (Stonnington STD-203) is an image with no text; not read.
3. AS/NZS 2566.1 (office copy is a scan) and the pipe makers' installation guides: not readable.
4. The office "Pipe Loads.xlsx" is steel pipe (ASME), so I did not use it.

## One recommendation
Store outside diameter by size and material: UPVC 110, 160, 250, 315, 400 (100 to 375); Stormpro 169, 259, 343, 428 (150 to 375); concrete 279, 362, 445 (225 to 375, standard class only). Measure cover as finished surface to top of pipe (invert plus inside diameter plus one wall), with the pavement cases from the underside. Leave 90 blank until Tim says.
