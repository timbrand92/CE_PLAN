## Two cancellation boundaries

**Cancel before accepting network change:** keep the previous confirmed network and authority basis. No imported candidate becomes live.

**Cancel the endpoint rebind after an earlier accepted removal:** do not pretend the old pit has returned. Keep the last confirmed/historical relationship recorded but visibly missing/unresolved against the current geometry. Cancel preserves that review state; undoing the earlier drawing operation is a separate qualified action. No unrelated source or authority value changes merely because the rebind was cancelled.

## Result and review meaning

A current calculation cannot silently use an erased physical connection as if still valid. Preserve the last result with its actual basis and mark the affected result/review status according to the confirmed topology change. Unknown replacement geometry or level cannot become a zero input. Merely receiving revision B had not itself changed the calculation basis.

The final issue review shows the unresolved connection if it remains. This does not introduce an unapproved universal plot prohibition; engineering disposition and mechanically complete output remain separate under the issue owner.
