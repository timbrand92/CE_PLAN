How quick each everyday task must be: 19 budgets

Tick only the ones you would change; an untouched card means the recommended answer stands

19 items

A pick is one click in the drawing. A click is one click in a window, or an Enter or Esc to finish. Typed is a figure you type. Scrolls are 0 for every task. The limit is the looser number; over the limit is not finished.
