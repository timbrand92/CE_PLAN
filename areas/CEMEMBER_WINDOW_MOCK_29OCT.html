How big will the beam draw?  TIM'S OPEN QUESTION - nothing is decided and nothing changes until you answer. Same beam (310UB32.0: 298 mm deep, 149 mm wide), same drawing scale 1:100.

OPTION AReal size  (what CEMEMBER does today, at every scale)
At 1:100 it draws 298 mm deep and 149 mm wide. On the printed A1 sheet it is 3.0 mm deep.
At 1:20 it draws 298 mm and prints 14.9 mm deep.
Good: true dimensions, so a dimension or a clash check reads right. Cost: small and hard to see at 1:100.
OPTION BAbout 5 times at 1:100  (what the old SBEAM does: size follows DIMSCALE / 20)
At 1:100 it draws 1490 mm deep and 745 mm wide (5 x). On the printed sheet it is 14.9 mm deep, the same as a real-size beam at 1:20.
At 1:20 it draws 298 mm: normal (1 x). At 1:50 it would be 2.5 x.
Good: readable at every scale. Cost: the drawing no longer holds the true size, so CE warns: "drawn 5 times its real size".
WARNINGWhat you would see (never blocks)
Beam size: 310UB32.0 is drawn 5 times its real size (1490 mm deep, real 298 mm) because the drawing scale is 1:100. Prints the same as at 1:20.
Shown amber in the window under Scale, on the command line when you Place, and as a line in the log. It never stops the beam being drawn. With Option A it shows "drawn at real size" in green and never turns amber.
The LTSCALE warning is separate: if LTSCALE is not 10 x the scale (for example 100 at 1:100), the dashes may look wrong, and the same amber band says so.
