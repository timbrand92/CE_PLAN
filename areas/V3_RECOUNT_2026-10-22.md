## 1. Method (same as recount 2) and where it is weak

1. **Denominator.** The same 548: 526 UX IDs (Codex's coverage index of `V3_UX_PLAN.md` plus the 25 Sep rulings) and 22 build-queue items. Split Civil 212, Manager 25, Shared 153, Structural 158.
2. **What I re-checked.** Every PR opened since 20 Oct 18:00 (36, #1487 to #1522); every PR whose head became an ancestor of an installed head since recount 2 (400 most recent PRs checked with `git merge-base --is-ancestor` against 6644752b / 9a8e5d4e then and 40ee0a36 / 2c9a9694 now; older PRs by the PR numbers in the merge commits); every merge commit on both installed lines since; the plan PRs named on 18 and 20 Oct (#1031, #1063, #1072, #1075 to #1077, #1080, #1111, #1113, #1119, #1123, #1208, #1246, #1248, #1250 to #1252), one by one: **none is installed.** No PR has ever been merged on GitHub; trains merge PR branches.
3. **Built, reachable and ON carry the 18 Oct estimates** and move only where a change since was found. That is the method's main weakness: the plan was not rebuilt item by item since 18 Oct.
4. **Whole items only.** A slice (a planner with no caller, a progress line without its Cancel) is not counted as built. Recount 2 did count "UX-UNDO-01, in part" as tested; I kept that for comparability.
5. **Robot check to plan item is my judgement.** The reo bar tool PASS is mapped to the basement reo bar item in UX-BSMT-01 to 06; the plan line does not split the six IDs.
6. **Tested is strict:** a robot PASS on the exact installed build. One skipped check (CDSYNC in run 47) moves an item out even though nothing broke.
7. **Accepted needs both a robot PASS and a Codex PASS.** A Codex line counts only when it says PASS (not DIFFERENT, BLOCKED, SEEN or NOT TESTED).
8. **Bugs are a status-line and name match.** "Awaiting" means the bug number appears in a commit message on the installed lines; some of those are tests, diagnostics or part fixes, so 463 is an upper limit. "Built not installed" means named only on another branch, an open PR title, or a status saying a fix is drafted. My parser reads `**Status:**` lines; 94 entries use other formats (for example "1. **Status / severity:** OPEN") and were read as open after a check that none of them records a closure.
9. **The carpark is stale in places.** BUG-858 says "not installed" but its fix #1510 is in Structural 1.0.121. BUG-859 is named on the installed line (#1520) but Codex V10 found it still fails (round 2, #1522, not installed); it sits in "awaiting" by the method.
