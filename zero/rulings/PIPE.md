# Current rulings: Pipe

Current rulings as of 20 Nov 2026 (Melbourne), from decisions D-001 to D-383 (D-298 to D-383 filed 20 Nov 2026). If this page and DECISIONS.md disagree, DECISIONS.md wins and this page is stale.

## What is decided now

### What a pipe is

1. **Its own element:** a pipe and its pits work together but are not one element (D-031, replacing the reading of D-011).
2. **Kinds in the first release:** new stormwater, charged, suspended and detention, with twin pipes as one pipe (THE_PLAN section 3, built on D-082 and D-083); trench grate, AG and perimeter drains are also in (D-223; see OTHER_DRAINS). **Narrowed for Stage 1 by D-377 item 1 (20 Nov):** "yes ordinary for now because theyre all the same visually so we will do the rest later, there is also the rising main for later": the ordinary pipe only in Stage 1; charged, suspended, detention, twin and the rising main later (Tim's room to confirm charged is out); the release itself is paused to one job (D-337, D-338, see FIRST_RELEASE).
3. **All pipes behave alike:** each part (invert level text and the rest) sits in one standard spot on every pipe kind, and slides along the pipe only to clear a clash (D-041).
4. **Bends on every kind of pipe,** gravity included (D-189).
5. **Typed name:** PIPE / CD, keeping today's CD as the one exception to "old names are gone" (D-057, D-101).

### Where it starts and stops

6. **Drawn from exactly where you click on the pit wall,** in line with the click (D-034). **Added by D-383 item 3 (20 Nov):** a click inside a pit starts the pipe on the wall facing the next click.
7. **Stops at the inner wall line:** the pipe passes the dashed outer line and stops at the solid inner wall, which differs from old sheets at pit edges (D-102).
8. **A branch joining a pipe with no pit is always allowed;** an inspection opening is offered, never forced, when the join is at a bend in the main pipe, the main line has no inspection opening and the run is long (D-081); the offer at a bend stands now that every pipe can bend (D-189).
9. **Loose pipes are allowed and are not an error:** what it cannot know reads "not known yet"; it shows quietly as "not joined yet" in grey; when its end is brought to a pit or pipe it joins and what can be worked out fills in (D-176, D-175).

### Its figures

10. **Material is pick-or-type,** and so is grade (D-079); pipe size is pick-or-type (D-080).
11. **Both invert levels can be typed over** (D-080), and under the foundation any held pair works out the third: hold upstream invert and grade, or downstream invert and grade, or both inverts (D-086).
12. **A new gravity pipe starts at 1 in 100,** which can be typed over (D-143). **Settled by D-263 (18 Nov):** a pipe starting from nothing opens at the office default (100 dia at 1 in 100); a pipe drawn off another opens with the size it continues (item 32).
13. **A new pipe opens at 100 mm everywhere;** it can always be changed and a council figure would raise it (D-174). **Refined by D-263 (18 Nov):** a pipe drawn off an existing pipe or pit opens with the size it continues, never smaller downstream (item 32). **Made specific by D-372 and D-373 (20 Nov):** a new pipe opens at the biggest pipe arriving; from nothing, or from a downpipe, tank or legal point, the office default 100 dia., a setting (items 55 and 56).
14. **Flatter than 1 in 200 on an ordinary gravity pipe is flagged amber** (D-142).
15. **Cover less than the minimum shows red "Wrong" in every window and never blocks** (D-140); the minimum cover follows a "what is over it" choice on each pipe (D-133; see LEVELS_AND_STANDARDS). **Carried into D-287 (18 Nov):** the choice is now one list of ten presets, a guide only that the engineer can change on any pipe. **How it is used changed by D-383 item 7 (20 Nov):** "what is over it" is not asked pipe by pipe; it opens "Not known" with no nagging, the cover check then reads grey "Cannot check yet" (control room's call); the presets stay as something he may set. **Short cover passes within the job's tolerance (D-321, D-350):** 5 mm by default, a setting.

### Detention pipe

16. **Detention is its own kind of pipe,** on its own layer, in a slightly different blue, with a line in the legend (D-083).
17. **Colour 160, deeper blue** (option A) (D-156, which replaced colour 152 of D-153).
18. **Layer STORMWATER DETENTION PIPE, linetype DASHED** like the ordinary pipe: shown to Tim with the colour pick and not objected to, but not separately ruled (D-153, D-156).
19. **Twin detention pipes:** one pipe that says "2 x 225", with a number-of-pipes box, two bands drawn, one label, and the storage sum counting both (D-082).
20. **Minimum grade for detention pipes:** 225 at 1 in 200, 300 at 1 in 250, larger at 1 in 300; a council figure overrides (D-134); a detention pipe smaller than 225 gets 1 in 200 flagged amber (D-147).
21. **Detention pipe cover:** keeps 300 (D-133), and under a driveway the larger figure, 0.45, governs (D-145). **POSSIBLE CONFLICT: detention pipe under a driveway.** D-145 says the larger, 0.45; D-287's presets say detention keeps 300 everywhere (driveway lines 100). Neither marks the other. Waits on: Tim. **"not sure need more research" (D-314, 19 Nov), then SETTLED by D-330 (19 Nov):** "300 paved, 450 unpaved (Recommended)": 300 mm to top of pipe under a driveway with concrete or paving, detention pipe or not; 450 mm where vehicles cross with no pavement; the picked council can override; the engineer can always change it. D-145's 0.45 m for a detention pipe under any driveway no longer holds (item 57).

### Label and editing

22. **The label prints the design grade as typed** ("1 IN 75"); inverts are still rounded to two decimals (D-154, which replaced D-123 for the label).
23. **A label moved by hand stays where it was put** when the pipe changes; the wording updates (D-014).
24. **The Pipe tool writes its own label;** the office's pipe description label blocks are dropped (D-229, "Drop them"). Confirmed by D-290: the label is text, not a block (item 39).
25. **A pipe label typed over by hand keeps the wording,** goes on the unfinished list as no longer matching, and one click lets it follow the pipe again (D-182).
26. **Placing:** type CD, the window opens every time already filled in, then draw (D-060, D-058); editing through Properties or double-click (D-061). **Refined by D-234 and D-263 (18 Nov):** the window opens when the name is typed, filled in as in item 32, one window per run, then pick after pick. **Superseded by D-354 and D-355 (20 Nov):** draw first, window second: type CD, click the run or pick a polyline, THEN the window opens knowing the run (item 52). Editing by Properties or double-click stands, with CDEDIT added (D-383 item 1).
