The job, stage by stage

Each sheet below is one stage of a job. The ribbon will follow this same order, and the CE panel shows the stages with a tick and one button each. It is a guide you can follow, never a set of steps you are forced through.

Civil

  • New job copies the A1 or A3 office template into the job folder and fills in the title block.
  • Job set-up: job details once, then council, land use, and markup or from scratch.
  • By 9 Novboth buttons first on the CE tab, and proven on a copy.

Structural

  • Job details and the new job drawing from the office template.
  • Readythe job record saved with the job, not on one PC (BUG-496).

House rules every CE command follows, Civil and Structural alike

One look.

Every window uses the New Pipe header and footer in gold, black and grey, all on one page.

One CE tab, in job order.

Each button shows its plain name and its command, for example "Pit Schedule (PITSCHEDULE)".

Old names still work.

Typing PIT or 1 still works, even when the button has moved.

Preview, then one Undo.

No "Are you sure?" after a preview. Escape leaves nothing half-drawn.

CE never saves your drawing.

It reminds you if autosave is off.

Every number lives on Settings.

Each one is marked council, job or office, with its source and edition. CE warns you; it never blocks you.

Looks like ours.

Schedule blocks, labels, symbols, text, layers and linetypes match your existing drawings, and a robot checks it.

Fast and solid.

Every command has a speed limit. It is tested on a 50,000-object drawing, locked layers, xrefs and bad input.

Your protections stay.

Locked layers, xrefs and REFEDIT are never bypassed.

Manager only manages.

Versions, updates, roll back and Report a problem. Drafting tools live on the CE tab.

At most 3 builders work at once, and at most 10 pieces wait for checking. Codex and the Claude accounts are paced so each week lasts until its reset. Something counts as accepted when every robot check passes, Codex has checked it in AutoCAD, and it has run 5 working days on your PC with no new bug. You can still reject anything.
