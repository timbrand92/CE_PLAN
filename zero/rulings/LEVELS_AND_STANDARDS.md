# Current rulings: Levels and standards (cover, grades, drops, pit sizes, step irons, slab, council against standard)

Current rulings as of 20 Nov 2026 (Melbourne), from decisions D-001 to D-383 (D-298 to D-383 filed 20 Nov 2026). If this page and DECISIONS.md disagree, DECISIONS.md wins and this page is stale.

## What is decided now

### Who wins

1. **When rules disagree:** council, then AS 3500.3, then the office guidebook (D-022).
2. **For cover, a council's own figure against the standard's table: take the larger of the two;** a council figure never lowers the standard's minimum (D-144, which narrows D-022 for cover).
3. **The plug-in never bends a standard by itself:** "Nothing: tell me, I decide" (D-124).
4. **Old council figures:** keep only sourced figures, with their source; the rest start blank and are filled in from the first real council letter (D-096); a council figure goes in only if its source document is on file (D-148).
5. **Standards fill a fact in and can be typed over;** a typed-over standard shows "typed over" with the standard's figure beside it (D-150; FOUNDATION).

### Cover

6. **Minimum pipe cover follows the standard's table by situation,** through a "what is over it" choice on each pipe: 100 single dwelling no traffic; 300 other sites no pavement; 450 under traffic no pavement; 100 under reinforced pavement; 75 under light pavement; detention pipe keeps 300 (D-133). **Carried into ten presets by D-287 (18 Nov):** the same idea, now one guide list with a lid class beside each line, engineer can change it (item 22). **How it is used changed by D-383 item 7 (20 Nov):** not asked pipe by pipe; opens "Not known" (item 34).
7. **A detention pipe under a driveway: the larger, 0.45;** detention is the kind of pipe and the driveway is what is over it, and the larger governs (D-145). **POSSIBLE CONFLICT: detention pipe under a driveway.** D-145 says the larger, 0.45; D-287's presets say detention keeps 300 (driveway lines 100). Neither marks the other. Waits on: Tim. **Superseded by D-330 (19 Nov), after D-314 "not sure need more research":** "300 paved, 450 unpaved (Recommended)"; D-145's 0.45 m no longer holds (item 30).
8. **Cover under paving (the 0.10 and 0.075 figures) is measured from the underside of the pavement** (D-146).
9. **Slab thickness for that is set "by what is over it",** a short editable list (D-152); the starting figures are DRAFT (see Still open). **Superseded by D-293 (18 Nov):** no office list and no starting figures; the thickness comes from each job's plans and is set per job (item 24). D-293 does not name D-152; the replacement is read from its content.
10. **Cover less than the minimum shows red "Wrong" in every window and never blocks** (D-140). **With a tolerance since D-318, D-321 and D-350 (19 and 20 Nov):** cover within the job's tolerance (5 mm by default) passes, reading "Within the 5 mm tolerance" (item 31).

### Grades

11. **A new gravity pipe starts at 1 in 100,** can be typed over (D-143). **Refined by D-263 (18 Nov):** from nothing, the office default (100 dia at 1 in 100); drawn off another pipe or pit, the size it continues, never smaller downstream.
12. **An ordinary gravity pipe flatter than 1 in 200 is flagged amber** (D-142).
13. **Detention pipe minimum grade, a stepped list by size:** 225 at 1 in 200, 300 at 1 in 250, larger at 1 in 300; a council's own figure overrides (D-134).
14. **A detention pipe smaller than 225: 1 in 200, flagged amber** "Needs a look: detention pipe smaller than 225" (D-147).

### Pits

15. **Drop across a pit (invert in to invert out): 0.02 office standard,** can be typed over on a pit (D-141, D-084).
16. **Steps in the orifice pit: office standard, can be typed over** (D-084).
17. **Pit size by depth, the guidebook table:** 350 to 0.45, 450 to 0.60, 600 to 0.90, 600 x 900 to 1.20 with step irons, 900 x 900 deeper than 1.20; a council condition overrides (D-135). **Changed by D-235 and D-272 (18 Nov):** the table is a guide offered when the depth is known; the starting size is not taken from the depth, and any pair the block takes is allowed (item 26).
18. **Step irons from 1.0 m** (the design checklist figure, not the 1.2 m in the sheet note) (D-136), shown amber "Needs a look" with no fourth status (D-149).

### Levels

19. **Cover levels are locked once typed** (D-078), **set by the engineer from house and pavement levels, never the survey** (D-178), and help is only ever a suggestion (D-195).
20. **Normally fixed on a tight site:** council connection invert, cover levels, top water level and orifice (D-018).
21. **A new pipe opens at 100 mm,** raised by a council figure (D-174). **Refined by D-263 (18 Nov):** from nothing, the office default (100 dia at 1 in 100); drawn off another pipe or pit, the size it continues, never smaller downstream.

### Added 18 Nov 2026, D-230 to D-297

Tim: "RIGHT IDEA, BUT FOOTPATHS MIGHT BE TRAVERESED BY VEHICLES IN CERTAIN CIRCUMSTANCES SO EVERYTHING WILL BE A GUIDE AND OR PRESET AND AN ENGINEER NEEDS TO HAVE ABILITY TO CHANGE". The ten lines (cover in mm, lid class): 1 garden, house, no vehicles 100, A; 2 garden or landscape, units and other sites, no vehicles 300, A; 3 footpath, paved, walkers only 75, A (a footpath vehicles can cross is the engineer's call: pick a driveway line or type over); 4 house driveway, concrete 100, C; 5 house driveway, pavers 75, C; 6 common driveway or carpark (units), concrete 100, C; 7 basement carpark or ramp 100, C; 8 truck hardstand, industrial 100, D; 9 vehicles, no paving 450, C; 10 council road or nature strip, the council's cover, D. The detention pipe keeps 300 everywhere; a council's own figure overrides a line (the larger governs, D-144). Sources: D-133, D-242, D-243, R-010. **POSSIBLE CONFLICT: detention pipe under a driveway.** D-145 says the larger, 0.45; D-287's presets say detention keeps 300 everywhere (driveway lines 100). Neither marks the other. Waits on: Tim. **Settled by D-330 (19 Nov) for the detention pipe;** whether D-330's "300 mm under a driveway with concrete or paving, detention pipe or not" also replaces lines 4 to 6 (100 and 75) is not said: see the INDEX clash list. **How the list is used changed by D-383 item 7 (20 Nov):** not asked pipe by pipe; opens "Not known"; the presets stay as something he may set.
23. **Lid class depends on where the pit sits (D-240, D-242, D-243):** pit by pit, not a job-wide setting; "Class C (office practice)" for a single house driveway, Class A for garden pits, "Class D" for a truck hardstand or industrial slab; a council's request overrides (see PIT item 32). **See the INDEX clash list:** D-383 item 7 says "what is over it" opens "Not known" and is not asked; whether a pit's lid class still opens at Class C or D by place is not said.
24. **Slab thickness under paving comes from each job's plans (D-293):** "should read from the plans and then you can set it in each jobs". No office preset: the thickness is set per job (in the Job window, per kind of paving) and typed over on a pipe where it differs; until it is set, cover under paving reads "Not known". Planning chat's reading of "read from the plans" (planning chat's call from his rulings; his to overturn): taken from the structural or architectural drawings by the engineer; the plug-in reading it off the drawing itself is an idea for later, not built.
25. **When cover is short on a run (D-280):** "Say how short, list the ways; no figure (Recommended)". The window says how short and where, then lists the ways out with what each would give (flatten a grade, reduce the drop at a pit, lower the connection); it never picks a number. The 0.01 suggestion goes.
26. **Pit sizes (D-235, D-272):** size from the depth is not the starting rule; any pair PIT BLOCK 3 takes is allowed (see PIT items 28 and 30).
27. **Grade limits (D-276 DQ-047; planning chat's call from his rulings; his to overturn):** a grade of 0 or below is Wrong, steeper than 1 in 1 Needs a look, anything else is held and printed as typed (D-079, D-154).
28. **Checks and limits (D-277, D-288; planning chat's call from his rulings; his to overturn):** "Cover needed" is shown, not typed, and a council's own figure is a line in the list (D-277 DQ-053, D-144); the "Needs a look" upper limits are amber only, never Wrong, each a line in the checks list: pit deeper than 6 m, roof over 5,000 m2, grade flatter than 1 in 500, pipe over 1,050 dia, site over 50,000 m2 (D-288 DQ-092 to DQ-096); DQ-098 CHANGES the build: a level below 0.00 is amber "check the datum" everywhere, the schedule included, never Wrong by itself (sites near the bay sit close to 0.00); DQ-099 zero or negative depth, area, diameter, length or volume is Wrong, a part bigger than the whole is Wrong, a coefficient above 1 is Wrong, and checks built on them read "Cannot check yet" in grey.
29. **Charged and suspended pipes (D-269):** "Charged as buried; suspended no check (Recommended)": a charged line gets the ordinary cover check by what is over it; a suspended pipe shows "no cover check: suspended" in grey.

### Added 20 Nov 2026, D-298 to D-383

30. **Pipe cover under a driveway (D-314, then D-330; evidence `reference\research\R-013`, checked at source):** "not sure need more research", then "300 paved, 450 unpaved (Recommended)". Preset to top of pipe: 300 mm under a driveway with concrete or paving, detention pipe or not; 450 mm where vehicles cross with no pavement; the picked council can override; the engineer can always change it. D-145's 0.45 m for a detention pipe under any driveway no longer holds.
31. **Checks pass within a tolerance (D-318, D-321, D-350, D-380):** "Allow a small tolerance" (D-318; the planning chat's draft call, exact figures only, is overturned); "A setting, opens at 5 mm" (D-321): the tolerance on levels and cover is a setting an engineer can change per job, in the Job window. A worked invert is checked at its exact figure with the job's tolerance: "Exact figure, with tolerance (Recommended)" (D-350): cover really 297 mm against 300 reads "Within the 5 mm tolerance"; with the tolerance at 0 it reads "3 mm short"; still printed to two places; the draft rule that checked the printed figure is withdrawn. A grade passes when it meets the required figure at two decimal places: the planning chat's call in D-321, **confirmed by Tim in D-380 (20 Nov)**.
32. **Garden runoff (D-315, D-331):** "Council's figure, else 0.35 (Recommended)" (see DETENTION item 35; evidence `reference\research\R-014`).
33. **One council's detention check (D-327):** at least 60 per cent of storage underground and an orifice no smaller than 65 mm, when that council is picked in the Job window (see DETENTION item 34).
34. **"What is over it" is not asked pipe by pipe (D-383 item 7, changing how D-133 and D-287 are used):** "we dont have to do this for every pipe it should be done automatically off plans or unkown if not known, its a minor feature or function that is simply a check". It opens "Not known" with no nagging; the presets stay as something he may set. Control room's call for what the cover check then says: grey "Cannot check yet", never amber.
35. **Ground for the cover check (D-383 item 6):** the pit levels plus points he adds.
36. **Levels along a run are in Stage 1 (D-383 item 8, D-359):** type one level, the rest worked out with the 0.02 drop (D-141).
37. **Rounding (D-341, confirmed by D-380):** every length and level is first taken to the nearest tenth of a millimetre, then rounded half up for display (see DRAWING_AND_SHEETS).
38. **With no Job window in Stage 1 (D-381 item 2):** office defaults: 5 mm tolerance, the ten presets, no council, text for 1:100; each window says so in one grey line.
