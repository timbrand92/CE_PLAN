**Status, 18 Nov 2026, 5:20 am: NOT YET READY TO HAND OVER.** Six windows are ready with cautions; Levels along a run is not. This page is finalised after the third independent trial. Written by the planning chat for checklist step 16 (Tim, D-087: "i cant give you full answer until using").

## What this is

Seven real program windows on a test bench. They run on the real brain, so every figure is worked out, not drawn in. It is NOT AutoCAD: the picture on the left is a simple stand-in for the drawing, and OK does not draw anything. Nothing is installed and nothing on your PC changes.

## How to open them

1. In File Explorer go to `C:\CE_CIVIL\bench\CeCivil.Bench\bin\Debug\net10.0-windows\`.
2. Double-click `CeCivil.Bench.exe`. A small chooser opens.
3. Pick a window. Close it with Cancel or the X to come back to the chooser.

## Ten minutes, in this order

1. **Pipe (2 min).** Type a new grade. Watch the downstream invert, the fall and the label follow. Click the padlock on the downstream invert, then type a new upstream invert: the grade is now the one worked out. Look for: does it say what else changed, in words you would use?
2. **Pit (1 min).** Type a deeper depth until the step-irons line shows. Pick a pit size from the list, then type one that is not on the list.
3. **Levels along a run (2 min, only once it is fixed).** Type 5.15 at the connection, then press "Flatten all grades to 1 in 200". Hold one pit's level and work uphill. Look for: is it clear what is held and what is worked out along the whole run?
4. **Inlets (1 min).** Change the kind (downpipe, overflow, floor waste). Type a roof area, then let it go back to the measured one with the padlock.
5. **Tank (1 min).** Pick 3,000 L, then 10,000 L. Look for: the note text, the top water level, and the plain-words warning when the tank is too small.
6. **Detention (2 min).** Change the paved area. Watch both answers side by side (Boyd's and OSD4W). Make the storage short and read what it says.
7. **Pit schedule (1 min).** Type over one cover level: the cell is held, shows a padlock, and the depth follows. Click the padlock to let it follow the pit again.

Then try to break one: a comma instead of a point, a negative number, letters. It should refuse in plain words and keep the old figure.
