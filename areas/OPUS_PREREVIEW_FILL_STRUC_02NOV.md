2. **The design read asked for (handle binding, no group).**
   1. **Sound for its purpose.** COPY, ARRAY, MIRROR with erase, COPYCLIP/PASTECLIP and INSERT-then-EXPLODE all give new handles, so `self=` no longer matches and TryReadMember refuses with "these lines look like a copy" (CeMemberRecordBinding.cs:76-80). AutoCAD does not reuse handles within a drawing, and save/reopen keeps them. A group adds nothing the handle check does not already give, so "no group" is acceptable. It departs from the card's text ("reusing the SBEAM store's group"); the card should be updated to match.
   2. **Rule slice (j) must follow (write it into the card now).** The copy keeps the original's member id. If SBEAMEDIT gathers "all lines with this member id" and passes them all to TryReadMember, the copy's lines fail the handle check and the ORIGINAL is refused too. Slice (j) must first keep only lines whose saved handle equals their real handle, then apply the count check. The stored data supports this; the reader's contract needs a test that says so.
   3. **Stale points after MOVE, ROTATE, STRETCH or grips.** Handles survive these, so the record passes, but the saved picked points are where the member WAS. Slice (j) must redraw from the lines' real position (or refuse when the lines no longer match the record). Not a slice (i) defect.
