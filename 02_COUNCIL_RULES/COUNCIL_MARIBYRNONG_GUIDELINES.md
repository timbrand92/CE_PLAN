# Maribyrnong — Stormwater Drainage Requirements / Drainage Approval Guideline

**15 September 2026.** Source: Maribyrnong City Council, *Stormwater Drainage Requirements Guidelines*
(*Drainage Approval Guideline*, Engineering Services – Permits), 13 pages. Undated in the file.

**This document resolves an "Unknown" the evidence review deliberately left open**, and finds a stored value
that contradicts the warning stored beside it.

---

## 1. The Unknown that is now answered

CE's stored Maribyrnong profile marks `tcMinutes` as **Assumed**, with this note:

> *"Unknown: permit states Tso = 6 min, not Tc. **No substitution of Tso into tcMinutes is made.**"*

That was careful and correct — a council letter gave a Tso of 6 minutes and the review refused to put it in
the Tc field. **This document gives both:**

> **Pre-development:** ARI **5 years** · C **0.4** · **Time of Concentration (tso) 6 minutes** · **Time of
> Concentration (tc) 10 minutes**

So `tcMinutes` should be **10** and `tsoMinutes` should be **6**. The stored 5 is neither.

**Where the 5 probably came from.** Maribyrnong publishes a *second*, different set of times, for general
drainage design rather than for detention:

> §1.4.2 Minimum times of concentration: **Commercial — 5 minutes · Residential — 7 minutes · Low density —
> 9 minutes**

**So this council has two Tc sets and they are not interchangeable** — 5/7/9 as design minimums by
development type, and 10/6 as the detention calculation pair. A single `tcMinutes` field cannot hold that,
which is the same shape problem recorded in the integration specification.

Also worth noting: **residential 7 and low density 9 are identical to Frankston's**, and commercial gets the
**shortest** time (5 minutes) — the Frankston direction, opposite to Kingston's. Another sign of a shared
base text.

## 2. A stored value that contradicts its own warning

| | |
|---|---|
| Stored field | `storageAri: 20` |
| Stored warning, beside it | *"Boyd's method 1 in 5 ARI PSD, **1 in 10 ARI storage**"* |
| Maribyrnong says | *"Storage Volume… sufficient for a **10 year ARI**"* — and separately, *"the overflow facility should be designed to pass the runoff generated by a **20-year** average recurrence interval storm"* |

**The 20 is the overflow storm, put into the storage field.** The warning next to it has been right all
along. `storageAri` should be **10**, and the 20-year overflow requirement has nowhere to live.

This is the third distinct kind of data error found by reading sources: a wrong value, a value copied from
another council, and now **a value taken from the wrong row of the same document**.

## 4. Design criteria in full

**Average recurrence intervals for the drainage system** (§1.4.1):

| Area | ARI |
|---|---|
| Residential | **5 year** or greater |
| Commercial and industrial | **10 year** at least |
| Main drain where surcharge would seriously affect private property | **10 year** |
| Major commercial (e.g. the Defence Site) | **25 year** |

**Detention (§1.4.3):**

| | Pre-development | Post-development |
|---|---|---|
| ARI | 5 years | 5 years |
| Coefficient of runoff | **0.4** | **0.75 minimum** (greater of weighted or 0.75) |
| tso | **6 minutes** | — |
| tc | **10 minutes** | — |

- Storage volume from **inflow and allowable outflow hydrographs**, sufficient for a **10 year ARI**
- **Overflow must pass a 20-year storm**, for higher intensities or a blocked orifice
- *"There should be **no net increase** in storm water runoff… Pre development discharge rate = post
  development discharge rate."*
- Flow control outlet between the storage and the Legal Point of Discharge
- **Orifice minimum 40 mm**, with a **litter screen**

**Detention is triggered** for all new single property developments needing planning approval in residential
zones with an **impervious fraction after development of 40%**.

**A fifth different trigger:** Boroondara 80% · Yarra 70% · Banyule 65% · **Maribyrnong 40%** · Banyule
carpark 35%. Maribyrnong's is by far the lowest, so detention is required on far more jobs there.

**Exception:** developments where the impervious area after development is **the same as or smaller than**
existing.

**Above-ground detention** will only be considered *"where the site has minimal grade"*.

Where an existing dwelling stays, it **must be connected to the OSD**.

## 5. Other requirements

- **Floor levels 300 mm above the highest calculated water level for a 1 in 100 year ARI design flow**
- Overland flow path designed so flows in excess of the 1 in 10 piped capacity are conveyed safely
- **Outlet to an open waterway: velocity no greater than 1.5 m/s**
- Pit covers to Council's standard drawings — **minimum Class C medium duty ("gatic")** unless noted; Class D
  heavy duty where required. Junction and side entry grates of an approved **"bike safe"** pattern with the
  same load class.
- **Pipe bedding: 75 mm compacted depth of 20 mm Class 2 fine crushed rock.** Backfill Class 2 FCR wet-mix,
  compacted to **98% modified dry density**
- **Pressurised (charged) systems** are allowed, discharging to kerb and channel or a Council pit —
  **surface grates must not be used with a pressurised system**. A rainwater tank is recommended alongside,
  with its overflow to an approved point of discharge.
- Older localities — **Braybrook and Maidstone** — contain easement drains
- Plans must show every existing street feature (driveways, pavements, pits, poles, Telstra pits, sewer
  manholes, trees) and all service authority assets from **Dial Before You Dig**
- Detention plans must show invert levels, design surface levels beside the device, **floor levels of all
  buildings existing and proposed**, cross sections of the device, existing and design drainage levels,
  downpipe locations, pipe sizes/types/gradients, and **pit sizes and types**

**A note on the standard cited.** Dual pump systems are required to accord with *"AS3500.3.2 Section 9"*.
`AS/NZS 3500.3.2` is the **older two-part designation** (Stormwater drainage — Acceptable solutions), and
Section 9 is the pre-2015 numbering for pumps. Yarra's checklist cites *"Section 9 of AS3500.3"* the same
way. **Recorded as an observation, not as a ruling on which edition Maribyrnong wants** — no edition year is
given anywhere in the document.

## 6. Questions

1. **Maribyrnong's detention uses a 10-minute time of concentration with a 6-minute time to the outlet.**
   We have 5 minutes recorded, which looks to have come from their *other* table — the 5-minute minimum for
   commercial drainage design, which is a different thing. Have Maribyrnong jobs been run at 5?
2. **Our stored storage storm for Maribyrnong is 1 in 20; Council's is 1 in 10.** The 20 is their *overflow*
   requirement, sitting in the wrong field — and the warning stored right beside it already says 10. Has any
   Maribyrnong job been given 1-in-20 storage?
3. **Detention is required at only 40% hard surface** in Maribyrnong — the lowest threshold of any council
   read, where Boroondara is 80% and Yarra 70%. Is that being checked, or is the threshold assumed?
4. **Their post-development coefficient has a floor of 0.75** — the highest floor seen (Boroondara's is
   0.65). Is that floor being applied?
5. **Above-ground detention only where the site has minimal grade.** Has that ever been the deciding factor
   on a Maribyrnong job?
