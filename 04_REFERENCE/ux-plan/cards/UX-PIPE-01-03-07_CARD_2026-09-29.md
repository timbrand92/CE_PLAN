1. **Which distance does auto-join use, 75 mm or the pit catch (outline + 100 mm, UX-PIT-02, 26 Sep)?** Today a new pipe end that
   lands inside a pit's catch reach joins that pit and is moved to the pit wall; if two pits touch, CE refuses to guess. That is
   already the "only one matching pit, never guess" half of UX-PIPE-02. The "75 mm" number exists only in the design pack.
   **Recommendation: keep the pit catch (outline + 100 mm, already a Settings value) for pits; use 75 mm only as a NEW Settings
   value for the second half (pipe ends).**
2. **"Or another pipe end" (UX-PIPE-02b): what should happen when a new pipe end lands within the distance of the end of another pipe
   with no pit there?** Today an end on the middle of a pipe becomes a branch junction (CD-BRANCH-01, main never split); an end
   meeting another pipe's end is only "proposed" for the drafter to confirm in CDCHECK. Snapping and linking pipe-to-pipe is a new
   link kind and touches the linking, snap and Undo code (Opus review). **Recommendation: pits already done; treat pipe ends as a
   separate later card that shows the snap in the preview and records one "joined to P4 end" link, behind a Settings switch.**
3. **UX-PIPE-07, where should "Keep as a free end" be remembered for the whole job?** Options: (a) in the drawing's connection
   decisions (current ledger; remembered per drawing, travels with the file, colleagues see it, but not across the job's other
