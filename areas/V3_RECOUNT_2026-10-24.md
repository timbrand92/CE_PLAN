## 1. Method (same as recount 3) and where it is weak

1. **Denominator.** The same 548: 526 UX IDs plus 22 build-queue items. Split: Civil 212, Manager 25, Shared 153, Structural 158. The 390 = Civil + Manager + Shared. The 237 = Civil + Manager.
2. **Built and installed carry recount 3's figures** and move only where a whole item changed. I checked every change on the installed lines since recount 3: 17 PRs in C-61 (#1522 to #1549), 23 in C-62 (#1552 to #1645) and 2 in S-42 (#1533, #1547), 42 in all. I checked them by ancestry against f7f7d551 and e6d597c0, and read their titles. None completes a whole plan item. The nearest are #1526 (Settings rows for existing switches), #1530 (Checks grade on the real pipe length) and #1531 (Pit Schedule depth to the pit floor). All three are engineering fixes or parts.
3. **Tested** means a robot, runner or Codex PASS in real AutoCAD on the exact installed build (3.0.72 / 1.1.72 / 1.0.122), for a plan item. That is the brief's wording. Recount 3 used the robot only, but every test I counted this time is a runner pass, so the strict robot-only figure is the same 5.
4. **Accepted** means a robot or runner PASS plus a Codex PASS of the same behaviour (RULES 4.5 and the 13.3 amendment), for a whole item. Parts do not count. I counted the one item even though both its passes are one build back (see 3c).
5. **Whole items only.** A slice or one half of an item is not counted. Recount 3 counted "UX-UNDO-01, in part" as tested, and I kept that kind of allowance for UX-STD-03 (runner R04 leaves out the Pit Schedule and CDREBUILD) and for UX-DI-03 (the switch-off half only).
6. **Matching a test to a plan item is my judgement.** Runner cases name their item on their PLAN_ITEM line, and I used those. Where an ID appears in `V3_UX_PLAN.md` section 1, I counted it as Shared (UX-STD-01b, UX-STD-03).
7. **Bugs: same parse as recount 3.** I read the first line of each entry that starts with "**Status". Closed means FIXED (but not "FIXED IN SOURCE", "FIXED IN CODE", or "awaiting Tim"), RETIRED, WONT FIX, NOT REPRODUCED, or "closed as". Awaiting means the bug number appears in a commit message on the installed lines. Built not installed means the number appears on any other branch, in an open PR title, or in a status that says the fix is drafted or built. Everything else is open with no fix. 66 entries have no status line in that form. None of them records a closure, so all 66 sit in the open classes.
8. **Open PRs: sorted by title with a rule.** A title naming a BUG number, starting "Fix", or naming HUNT, privacy-audit or NIT work counts as a bug fix. Tests, guards, audits, trains and docs count as other. Everything else counts as plan work, including Settings rows, Level 3 slices, engines with no caller, and CEMEMBER. Treat it as a sorting rule, not a reading of each PR.
