## Evidence standard for closure

1. New supplements must be linked and explicitly take precedence over generic summaries where they correct them.
2. Inherited canonical decisions remain binding even where compressed into a grouped row. Later scoped approvals must be cited where they change an older rule.
3. Configured/current-source behavior, approved target and proposed refinements must remain separate.
4. Full declaration/registry coverage needs reconciled unknowns and discovery actions, not an invented daily status.
5. Any remaining design choice is recorded as open; it must not be answered by silence or by a browser prototype.
