## 1. Out of date, newer version found

1. **Kingston, Civil Design Requirements Part A.**
   **Held:** Issue 5 dated 11 Sep 2018 (file `Civil-Design-requirements-for-Developers-Part-A.pdf`) and Issue 4 dated
   23 May 2016 (file is named "Version 4.4" but its cover says Issue 4, May 2016). Also Road and Drains Design Standards,
   Issue 2 Rev 2, 21 Aug 2001.
   **Newer:** Issue 5 dated 26 March 2025. https://www.kingston.vic.gov.au/files/sharedassets/public/v/2/civil-design-requirements-for-developers-part-a-integrated-stormwater-management-issue-5.pdf
   Kingston's approvals page still says only "Issue 5" with no date. Part B Roadworks Construction Plan Requirements
   (Nov 2012) is also online and not held.
   **Why it matters:** the 2018 and 2025 documents are both called "Issue 5", so a label match proves nothing. The 2025
   one is the one that names AS/NZS 3500.3:2021 and writes storms as AEP (20% AEP residential, runoff coefficient 0.6,
   225 mm minimum council pipe, 2000 L tank per dwelling). Anything CE took from the 2018 copy needs re-checking.

2. **Bayside (Victoria), stormwater drainage design.**
   **Held:** BCC Stormwater Drainage Design Guidelines Version 2.0, May 2024. This was the Feb 2025 consultation draft.
   **Newer:** Developer Drainage Design Guidelines 2026 (Word file; no version or date printed on the page, upload folder
   is 2026-03). https://www.bayside.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-03/Developer%20Drainage%20Design%20Guideline%202026.docx
   And Developer Drainage Design Policy 2026 (PDF). https://www.bayside.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-03/Developer%20Drainage%20Design%20Policy%202026.PDF
   Both are linked from https://www.bayside.vic.gov.au/services/planning-and-building/legal-point-discharge
   **Why it matters:** this is the finalised guideline that `COUNCIL_BAYSIDE_2026.md` says CE needs and does not have.
   The numbers (permissible discharge, detention volume) live in it, not in the policy. Needs Tim's approval to download.
   Also online and not held: Subterranean Drainage Guidelines 2018 (bayside.vic.gov.au, 2021-10 upload).

3. **Darebin, Legal Point of Discharge Guidelines.**
   **Held:** February 2019 (revision history ends at version 01, 05/02/19), plus 2017 standard drawings.
   **Newer:** June 2025 (revision 02, 19/06/2025 per `COUNCIL_DAREBIN_LPD_2025.md`). https://www.darebin.vic.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/1/planning-and-building/documents/darebin-design-and-legal-point-of-discharge-guidelinespdf.pdf
   The OSD checklist is also online: https://www.darebin.vic.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/1/planning-and-building/documents/onsite-stormwater-detention-osd-checklist.pdf
   **Why it matters:** the 2025 edition has orifice pipes only (no plates), 100 and 150 mm pipes not allowed for OSD,
   minimum pipe sizes of 225 mm in easements and 300 mm under roads, 150 mm SN8 under slab, and connections into the top
   third of the pipe (DS14). The 2019 copy gives none of these.

4. **City of Yarra, Drainage Design Guidelines (DDG). Tim holds none.**
   **Held:** the "Yarra" folder is Yarra Ranges Council (see section 4), not City of Yarra.
   **Newer:** two files online, June 2026 and July 2026. https://www.yarracity.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-07/drainage_design_guidelines_ddg.pdf
   and https://www.yarracity.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-06/drainage_design_guidelines_ddg.pdf
   (search snippets only; the council site refused a direct fetch). The July file is the one CE_PLAN read (26 pages).
   **Why it matters:** CE_PLAN's Yarra rules (orifice Cd 0.6, 70% hard-surface detention trigger) come from a document
   that is not in Tim's folder. Which of the two 2026 files is the live one is not confirmed.

5. **Whittlesea, urban development guidelines.**
   **Held:** Guidelines for Urban Development, V2 Final, December 2015 (plus an IFD file dated 2019).
   **Newer:** Neighbourhood Design Manual, Engineering Guidelines, Version 1.0, September 2023. https://www.whittlesea.vic.gov.au/files/assets/public/v/2/documents/services/building-planning-and-development/neighbourhood-design-manual/neighbourhood-design-manual-engineering-guidelines.pdf
   **Why it matters:** it adopts EDCM Section 13 for drainage, sets permissible discharge at the 20% AEP storm, and
   holds storage 300 mm below habitable floors. Caution: Whittlesea's page still lists the old Guidelines manual and
   does not say the new one replaces it, so treat 2023 as probably current, not proven.

6. **VicRoads, Supplement to AGRD Part 3 Geometric Design.**
   **Held:** cover sheet January 2017 (supplement to the 2016 Austroads Part 3), file dated Aug 2020.
   **Newer:** Department of Transport supplement Version 5.0, November 2021. https://www.vicroads.vic.gov.au/-/media/files/technical-documents-new/supplements-to-the-austroads-guide-to-road-design/dot-supplement-to-agrd-part-3--geometric-design-v50.ashx
   Austroads has also updated Part 3 since (Edition 3.4 Feb 2021, and a June 2026 update); whether Victoria has adopted
   the June 2026 changes is not shown.
   **Why it matters:** the held supplement predates the 2021 Austroads edition it now sits on.

7. **Melbourne Water.**
   **Held:** a web print of "Hydrologic and hydraulic design" dated 11 Jan 2018, plus a 2020 build-over-easement guide
   and 2016/2022 pipe connection drawings.
   **Newer:** the live page exists at https://www.melbournewater.com.au/building-and-works/developer-guides-and-resources/standards-and-specifications/hydrologic-and
   (fetch refused, so I could not compare its text). Newer related documents: Development Application Requirements
   Guideline, version 2, July 2025 (https://www.melbournewater.com.au/sites/default/files/2025-07/Melbourne-Water-Development-Application-Requirements.pdf),
   an updated MUSIC guideline (2026), and the Flood Impact (Afflux) Practice Note v1 (2025).
   **Why it matters:** a 2018 print cannot be trusted for freeboard (0.3 m flowpath, 0.6 m waterway), minimum pipe
   (300 mm) or velocity limits without checking the live page. Afflux is now zero in floodplains.
