# Typed names: one strategy (draft for Tim to pick, not decided)

**Why:** Tim, D-046 and D-049: long names are hard to reuse; "THE TYPED NAMES NEEDS A REFRESH AND IT NEEDS TO BE ALL ON ONE STRATEGY". Today the Civil program alone has 145 typed names with no single rule (TOOLS_TODAY Part 4). Ribbon B is chosen (D-051), which is what makes a short list possible.

## The three strategies shown to Tim (page `pictures\pick_a_naming_rule.html`)

1. **A, "Type the thing" (recommended).** You only type a name to PLACE a thing or open a sheet job. The name is what the office already writes for that thing, two to four letters. Everything you do TO a thing has no typed name: click the thing, and its tab, grips and Properties do it. Every ribbon button shows its typed name in its tip.
2. **B, one prefix on everything.** Every name starts with the same letters (as most do today with CD). Never clashes with AutoCAD; names get long and alike.
3. **C, keep today's names.** Nothing to relearn; no rule; 145 names; three sit on AutoCAD's own short names (C, IO, RC).
