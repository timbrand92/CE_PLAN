5 of 10 (Q-L5)

Section and grid extras

SECTION TAG (12 jobs) beside SECTION MARK, and 9GRID (fine centre line, 10 jobs) beside GRID.

The section tag is the bubble on a detail and the mark is the cut on a plan; 9GRID is a light grid, GRID the main one. The source marks this "to confirm".
