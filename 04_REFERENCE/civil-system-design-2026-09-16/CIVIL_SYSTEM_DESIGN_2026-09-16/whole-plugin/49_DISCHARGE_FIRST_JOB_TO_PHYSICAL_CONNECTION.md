## During design

Select the outlet record first, then use its owner-qualified **Select connection on plan** route. Preserve source values and pending edits during the pick. Preview exact physical pipe endpoint/pit/target identity and affected branch; distinguish selected endpoint from the separate annotation position. A nearby pipe or first available branch is only a candidate, never silently the connection.

Compare physical endpoint level/role/datum with the adopted authority boundary. A disagreement is shown with both sources. Do not move or redesign council/existing infrastructure to make values agree. Fixed/Designed classification and deliberate unlocking remain separate from evidence adoption. A location label alone does not establish permission to discharge there.

Confirm the reviewed relationship through the existing shared authority. CDDESIGN, CDLPD detail and Drainage Items must then display the same selected outlet and evidence state. Separate unknown/unloaded source, pending relationship and physical mismatch rather than compressing them into a green complete label.

Cancel the pending pick retains the prior outlet record and known relationship. If an existing command has already accepted an immediate label/drawing action, expose that actual effect; cancelling later review does not claim to erase it. This supplements rather than repeats root32's drawing-return contract.

## Revision and final check

A newer authority letter or changed endpoint proposes a comparison against the affected outlet only. Retain the previous confirmed evidence and fixed boundary while Tim reviews it. Changed membership identifies the actual affected branches/calculations; other outlet records do not inherit that change automatically. A moved label is presentation, not a new discharge location or endpoint level.

Before issue review, show selected outlet, physical endpoint/branch, adopted authority evidence and remaining disagreements together. Shared QA decides issue disposition; this record introduces no new universal issue block. Source acceptance is not independent certification of legal permission beyond the actual document's wording/status.
