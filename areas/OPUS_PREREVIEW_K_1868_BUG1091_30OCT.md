### 3. Interaction with the 1 in 100 move (#1841), the Grade mismatch window (#1873, B-057) and cover/CDRULES

1. **1 in 100 table (#1841):** no conflict. The ordinary 1 in 100 rows reach the redesign exactly as before. The PR adds only large keys; adding a minimum can only steepen a proposal (`PipeSizingSolver.cs` 240-242 sets the grade to the minimum when the computed one is flatter). So **the redesign can never now produce a pipe flatter than the row, and CDRULES's minimum-grade row cannot newly fail.** CDRULES warns only when actual is flatter than minimum times (1 + 1e-6) (`RulebookEngine.cs` 414-420). One thing I could not verify: whether redesign rounds the levels it writes in a direction that could leave a 1 in 200 pipe at 1 in 200.4 ("just flatter"). I found no rounding in `NetworkDesignService.cs`, `NetworkRedesignSolverService.cs` or `NetworkRedesignApplyCoordinator.cs`, but I did not trace the level text written to the pits. The same question applies today to every ordinary 1 in 100 pipe, so it is not new.
2. **Grade mismatch window:** the window compares a typed "Set my own" grade with the grade the two levels give, more than 10 % apart (`CdCommand.Grade.cs` 233-260, called at `CdCommand.cs` 586-591). It does not read the minimum-fall list, so the PR does not feed it directly. **Indirect path, real:** with the row filled, New Pipe now fills in the downstream level of a 450 at the row's grade and switches it to "Set my own 200" (`CdViewModel.Inverts.cs` 786, 807-829). With `pipe.suggestedLevelNeverFlatterThanGrade` ON (BUG-1229, on C-77/C-78) that level is rounded down to 10 mm. Example: 4.1 m at 1 in 200 is a 20.5 mm fall, rounded to 30 mm, which is 1 in 137, 32 % steeper, so **the Grade mismatch window opens on placement.** A 30 m pipe (150 mm exactly) does not. That is the same nuisance #1873 fixes for ordinary pipes. #1873's skip rule (`Rules/MismatchBoxOwnSuggestionRule.cs` in `75e3baab`, `SkipsQuestion`) does not look at size, so it covers 450s too. Read, not run.
3. **Cover and CDRULES cover rows:** the network design builds levels from the outfall upwards (`NetworkDesignService.cs` 351-361: upstream level = downstream + length / N). A 450 held at 1 in 200 instead of about 1 in 890 lifts its upstream end by about 116 mm over 30 m, and every pit and pipe above it by the same. So **redesign and CDRUNSOLVE can now report "COVER: ... < minimum" on pits upstream of a big pipe, and CDRULES's cover rows can move from Pass to Warn or Unknown there.** Engineering-correct (it is Tim's figure), but a drafter will see more cover warnings on flat sites. In keep-sizes redesign a 450 that cannot meet both 1 in 200 and cover is reported as not achievable at its size rather than proposed flatter.
4. **Detention pipes:** CDRULES judges a detention pipe of the guide size and up in the four councils against R-32 (1 in 200) and elsewhere against the large row once filled (`RulebookEngine.cs` 390-401). The redesign holds it to the large row. With any row figure the proposal is steeper than before, so it can only move towards passing. Not tested.
