# Framing plans — CE practice (DRAFT, 26 Sep 2026)

Read-only study approved by Tim: framing-plan pages from 5 structural sets (2023-2025) read in full, 2 more scanned.
Anonymised (DATA-01). Items 1, 3, 6 and 8 CONFIRMED by Tim, 26 Sep 2026 (decisions file); the rest is a reading of the evidence.

1. **Beam tags:** category prefix + number: FB floor beam, RB roof beam, LRB lower roof beam, PB pergola/pavilion
   beam, AB (a ground/subfloor category), RL roof lintel, RT roof truss girder; a leading digit sometimes (e.g. 1FB5),
   probably the level. Modifiers appended: CANT, CONT, (O) existing, UNDER/OVER/BELOW.
2. **Lintels:** L1, L2...; roof lintels RL.
3. **Posts and columns:** C steel columns (SC stub column), P timber posts, DS1 double stud, TS1 triple stud.
4. **Rafters and joists:** R rafters, RT trusses, FJ floor joists.
5. **Hangers, straps, tie-downs:** no plan tags; general notes and standard details.
6. **Bracing walls:** legend symbols, not tags: Type A (strap brace) and Type B (ply brace) referring to lettered
   details; proprietary bracing symbol on some jobs.
7. **Legend:** "LEGEND" by the title area; "symbol + DENOTES ... + REFER TO ..." entries: bracing types, existing to
   retain/demolish, loadbearing walls under, double studs, floor/level over outline, trusses to manufacturer, CANT/CONT
   explained, set-downs, articulation joints.
8. **Member schedule:** "MEMBER SCHEDULE" / "ROOF MEMBER SCHEDULE" on the same framing sheet, columns MARK and SIZE,
   grouped by function (roof beams, roof lintels, lower roof beams, rafters/purlins, supports), spec notes appended to
   the size.
9. **Notes:** a numbered 1-5 block (AS 1684 for unspecified members, bracing and tie-downs to AS 1684, galvanised
   steel, open-web joist bracing, Pryda or equal connectors); truss manufacturer note; double studs under all beam and
   lintel ends; F17 KDHW may be substituted by equivalent LVL; read with architectural documents.
10. **Plugin gaps:** no MARK/SIZE member-schedule generator, no Type A/B bracing symbol tool, no hanger/strap tag tool.
11. **Tim, 26 Sep:** AB awning beam (tentative); TR trimmer beam; BJ balcony joist; SB subfloor beam; WT wall tie; a
    leading number = floor level or townhouse/unit number. Schedules show each full name, grouped under headings.
