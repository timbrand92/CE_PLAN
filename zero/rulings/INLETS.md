# Current rulings: Inlets (downpipe, overflow, FO, inspection opening, GIO, floor waste, trench grate)

Current rulings as of 18 Nov 2026, 5:30 pm (Melbourne), from decisions D-001 to D-297. If this page and DECISIONS.md disagree, DECISIONS.md wins and this page is stale.

## What is decided now

### Which inlets, and their names

1. **In the first release:** "Downpipe and the other inlets" (D-097), and trench grate (D-223).
2. **Typed names:** DOWNPIPE / DP, OVERFLOW / OF, FO / FO, INSPECTION / IO, GIO / GIO, FLOORWASTE / FW (D-101); each still to be proven free in AutoCAD 2027.
3. **FO has no longer name:** "Leave it as FO only" (D-059).
4. **IO keeps its name** although it sits on AutoCAD's own IO short name: "Keep IO" (D-054).

### Placing and moving

5. **An inlet such as a GIO goes exactly where the drafter clicks along the pipe,** slides along the pipe by its grip afterwards, and can also be placed off a pipe (D-050; the last two parts are the planning chat's answer given to Tim the same day).
6. **Placing many, for example 12 downpipes:** one window, then pick after pick, each numbered in turn, Enter to finish (D-186).
7. **An inspection opening is offered, never forced,** at a branch that joins a bend in the main pipe on a long run with no inspection opening (D-081).

### The Inlet window

8. **Square preview style** for a thing at one spot (D-067; the planning chat's sorting put downpipe, overflow, FO, IO, GIO and floor waste here, Tim may correct). **Superseded by D-296 (18 Nov):** the preview sits at the left in every window, so the square and wide styles no longer decide where it sits; its size and form are OPEN under D-297; the look is the approved style (D-295).
9. **"Tick: right track, same as the others"** and "yep happy witht hat window"; not a final acceptance, and symbols are to be the office's own blocks when built (D-111, D-112).
10. **On the bench the Inlet window looks good** (D-169).
11. **The inlet symbols are "The WITH MASK set":** the dynamic masked downpipe, IO, GIO, overflow, grated overflow and floor waste, from above and to below; the older floor waste blocks leave the palette (D-229).
12. **The inlet does not know where the house wall is;** the wall on the bench is made-up scenery (D-162, answer by the planning chat to Tim's question).

### Added 18 Nov 2026, D-230 to D-297

13. **Nine kinds in the one Inlet window (D-279):** "Grated overflow, From above, To below" all ticked, so the kinds are DP, OF, FO, IO, GIO, FW, grated overflow, from above and to below.
14. **Trench grate keeps TG (D-233):** "Keep TG; it takes over when the old plugin goes". Until the old plug-in is removed the new tool is tested under its full word TRENCHGRATE (TG is the old plug-in's trench grate command today).
15. **The pit and inlet blocks are not in the Blocks palette (D-232):** Tim edited the palette picture and took out the pit and inlet blocks, the strip drain label and the trench grate ("covered in the plugin"); the rest stay in the master drawing.
16. **What the Inlet window opens with (D-263):** inlets open with the last used choices, kept between AutoCAD sessions in a small CE file of its own; a setting lets anyone switch the tool to "always office default" or "always last used".
17. **Build calls on inlets (D-277, D-288; planning chat's call from his rulings; his to overturn):** DQ-056 inlet sizes 90, 100 and 150 offered, any other typed; DQ-057 an inlet bigger than its pipe is "Needs a look"; DQ-058 an inlet typed past the end of its pipe is Wrong, said in words, the typed figure kept; DQ-059 roof area applies to a downpipe only; DQ-086 a downpipe's arriving level at a pit is typed and held, and until typed it reads "Not known".

## Changed along the way

1. **TRENCHGRATE / TG as an inlet was only a recommendation (FUNCTION_MAP FM-032); D-223 (18 Nov) put trench grate in the first release.** Whether it is an inlet or a kind of pipe, and its typed name, are still open (D-067 sorted trench grate into the wide, along-a-line style).
2. **The square preview style (D-067) was replaced for where the preview sits by D-296 (18 Nov).**
3. **TRENCHGRATE / TG as a draft name was settled by D-233 (18 Nov):** TG, with TRENCHGRATE as the test name until the old plug-in is gone.
