# R-017 Which pipe material goes with each size

**Written:** Fri 20 Nov 2026, evening (PC clock), by Tim's room. **Feeds:** N-054 (the material that follows the size above 150); D-239.

**What was checked and what was not.** Checked at source by Tim's room: the office council checklist (a Word document) for the wording "STORMPRO PIPES for all internal detention in standard sizes" and the sewer class line. Everything else below was read by a research helper on the Team account and has NOT been re-read by the room. Sources are named in words; no web addresses. Not yet put to Tim.

## The question
What material and class does the office write for 90, 100, 150, 225, 300 and 375 dia. pipes, how often, and when?

## What was found
1. **100 dia: UPVC is the default.** Design Checklist workbook ("Civil Design Checklists.xlsx"): "all pipes 100 dia uPVC U.N.O." (appears on two sheets of the workbook). Office guidebook v1.3 thumb rules (section 7.1.18, page 48): "Minimum underground pipe diameter - 100 DIA". Read myself.
2. **100 dia: "sewer class" when it goes to the legal point or crosses a driveway.** Kingston drainage guideline (office Word file, design approach list): "Connection to council LPD via 100 DIA upvc sewer class at min 1 in 100". One council's compliance sheet asks "100mm diameter (UPVC sewerquality SN10 at grade 1 in 100)" for the kerb connection. Read myself. Driveway sewer-quality wording is only second-hand (digest LIMITS.md L-0xx, from a council letter and a checklist).
3. **100 dia: charged pipe to a tank is plain UPVC.** Kingston guideline: "Min 100 Dia upvc (charged pipes as required) to Water tank". Read myself.
4. **150 dia: UPVC SN8 under a garage, with an inspection opening each side.** Design Checklist workbook ("pipes under the garage to be 150 DIA uPVC SN8 with I.O.s on either side", two sheets). Guidebook v1.3 council RFI lists for Kingston, Glen Eira, Bayside, Monash and Frankston: all five say "All pipes under the garage to be 150 DIA UPVC SN8". Kingston guideline: "Min 150 DIA upvc SN8 under garage slab" and "Charged pipes under slabs to be sleeved inside 150 DIA upvc SN8 pipe". Read myself.
5. **150 dia: "SN8" is also the general rule for trafficked or shallow pipe.** Guidebook thumb rules, page 48: "Minimum pipe quality - Sewer quality/ SN8 for most applications (trafficable areas, areas with 100-300mm cover". Read myself.
6. **Issued-sheet label wording (three issued job sheets, via the digest STUDY_A, section 6 item 6 and pipe_sizes.csv).** "100 DIA. UPVC PIPE AT 1 IN 100", "150 DIA. UPVC SN8 PIPE AT 1 IN 100", "100 DIA. SEWER CLASS PIPE AT 1 IN 100", "100 DIA. UPVC PIPE CHARGED TO WATER TANK", "150 DIA. UPVC CHARGED PIPE TO WT", "150 DIA. UPVC SUSPENDED PIPE AT 1 IN 100 MIN." Second-hand: I did not open the drawings.
7. **225, 300, 375 dia: Stormpro for detention.** Guidebook page 18: "the underground detention pipes are STORMPRO or equivalent PVC Pipes". Kingston guideline: "STORMPRO PIPES for all internal detention in standard sizes", then 225, 300, and "375, 450, 525 DIA and above". Issued-sheet label (digest): "2 x 225 DIA. STORMPRO PIPES AT 1 IN 200" with "9m DETENTION". Read myself (guidebook, guideline); label is second-hand.
8. **Concrete (RCP) appears only as a council or road rule.** Digest LIMITS.md: concrete pipe, not Stormpro, in the road (one council); 300 rubber-ring-jointed concrete pipe to a council pit (one discharge advice); digest R-013: one council's table lists RCP and sewer-quality uPVC separately. Second-hand. No RCP wording on an office label was found.
9. **Rules for "when".** Under a garage: 150 SN8 (items 4). Between pits: one council's checklist wants 150 minimum (digest pipe_sizes.csv; second-hand). To the legal point: 100 sewer class (item 2). Detention: Stormpro (item 7).

## Not found / could not read
1. **90 dia:** no office label, rule or sheet mentions it. Not found.
2. **Counts of each label across issued sheets:** Not found. Issued drawings (DWG) are not in the two permitted folders; the digests quote wording from three sheets without counts. Counts above are mentions in documents, not labels on sheets.
3. **A 300 or 375 label wording:** only the detention rule, no quoted label. Not found.
4. **Guidebook pictures** other than pages 18 and 19 were not viewed.

## One recommendation
Fill the material from size with: 90 not set (ask Tim); 100 UPVC; 150 UPVC SN8; 225, 300, 375 Stormpro. Offer "SEWER CLASS" for 100 as a choice (legal point or driveway), and keep RCP as a manual pick only.
