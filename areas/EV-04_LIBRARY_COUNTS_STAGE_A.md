## 5. Construction methods: not readable from words, and why

1. **Footing plans name several systems each.** Of 69 jobs with a structural set, the footing plan sheets name: 0 systems on 15 jobs; 1 systems on 5 jobs; 2 systems on 8 jobs; 3 systems on 18 jobs; 4 systems on 16 jobs; 5 systems on 4 jobs; 6 systems on 3 jobs.
2. **That is the standard legend and notes,** which list piers, strip, pad, raft and stumps whether used or not. Counting words would say every job uses everything.
3. **Only 5 jobs name exactly one footing system:** Stiffened raft 2; Waffle raft 1; Strip footing 1; Bored piers 1. Too few to count by type.
4. **Which system was actually used is drawn, not written:** the hatch, the pier symbols, the beam lines on the footing plan. Reading that needs the drawings (the AutoCAD core-console method of Evidence Jobs 1 and 2), not the PDFs.
5. **So every construction method stays Unknown for stage A.** No number is given rather than a misleading one.

## 7. What could not be read, and why

1. **162 of 889 sheets gave no readable title** (153 structural, 9 civil). The title block uses a layout the reader does not recognise, or its text is drawn as lines. They count as "not found".
2. **3 jobs gave no title on any sheet.** They are still in every "of" total.
3. **20 sheets had no text at all** (picture-only PDFs).
4. **PDFs that failed to open: 0.** Every one of the 78 jobs was read. Zero silent skips.
