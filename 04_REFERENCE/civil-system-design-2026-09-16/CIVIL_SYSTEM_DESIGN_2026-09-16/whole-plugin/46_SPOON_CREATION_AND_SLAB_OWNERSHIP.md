## Later change

Moving a spoon retains its confirmed parent identity. If its new location makes that relationship geometrically inconsistent, show Needs review against that parent; do not attach it to whichever slab now contains it. Any deliberate reassignment is a separately reviewed proposal naming old/new slab and affected children/surface previews, not an approved automatic rule.

Moving a slab follows its approved compound ownership; referenced survey/pit evidence does not become a child merely because it supplied a level. Editing a spoon outline changes the relevant slab preview basis. A manual annotation move alone changes presentation. Copying must not leave two distinct children sharing one permanent identity or silently acquire a new parent; exact copy choice remains with the reviewed owner.

Cancel before final outline creation attempts temporary cleanup in the observed source, but earlier sync/layer effects and swallowed erase failure must be qualified. After creation, later parent-review Cancel preserves honest accepted/unassigned state. A partial failure reports actual outline, hatch and relationship separately, and retry follows readback rather than automatic duplication.
