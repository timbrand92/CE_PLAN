## 1. Method

1. **How the figures arrive:** the discharge letter gives the permitted discharge when there is one (5 L/sec on CIV-02; 6 L/s on CIV-03, in both the letter and the permit condition).
2. **No figure, no detention:** a letter typed "single unit development" with NO permitted discharge figure means no detention sum (CIV-01).
3. **Volume:** Boyd's on the council's own copy of the spreadsheet (S-003).
4. **Discharge options in the letter:** kerb by gravity or pump; existing council pit in the nature strip; on-site infiltration subject to soil.
