# R-015 — Real plan sizes of rainwater tanks sold in Victoria

## Makers' sizes and shapes, checked 19 Nov 2026

Public web only (Melbourne, 19 Nov 2026). Sizes in mm, written length x width x height. Round tanks are diameter x height. "Checked at source" means I opened the maker's or seller's own page or document and read the figure there. "Not checked at source" means a search summary only, or I was blocked. No client data used.

### ANSWER FIRST

1. **Plan shape: I could not confirm any slimline plan shape at source.** No maker page or sheet I could read states "rounded ends" or a corner radius. The Tankworld sheets are drawings, but I could not view the drawing part. So every slimline shape below is "not checked at source".
2. **A 3,500 L slimline does exist, with a checked size.** Rotoplus 3,500 L Super Shortbase Slimline: 2380 x 870 x 2150 (ASC Water Tanks product page, which also lists the volume as 3,500 L).
3. **Steel slimline is made to order.** The Tank Factory page says all its steel tanks are made to order, the listed sizes are the best-value ones, and made-to-measure tanks are available by contacting them with the dimensions wanted. It publishes no rule for length, width or height limits.
4. **The stated litres are lower than the box volume.** Steel: about 90 to 93 percent of length x width x height. Poly: the Rotoplus 3,500 L comes to about 79 percent. So never work litres out from the box (my own arithmetic, not a maker figure).

## Checked at source, 19 Nov 2026

Checked against the makers' drawings in the office Tanks folder and HB 230-2008 (Rainwater Tank Design and Installation Handbook, office copy).

### What the office sources hold

1. **All the office's tank drawings are one maker's precast concrete "Universal" tanks (Icon-Septech).** They are round, below-ground tanks (buried, with access openings at the lid). None are slimline or above-ground poly or steel tanks. So none of the earlier figures (Polymaster, Tank Factory, ASC/Rotoplus, Bushman, Tankworld, Kingspan) could be checked against office documents.
2. **HB 230-2008 gives no tank plan sizes, pad sizes or oversize figures.** Clause 5.15 sets rules for underground rainwater tanks: lid strong enough for people, child-proof lids, overflow protected from stormwater surcharge, hold-down against flotation, drainage to AS/NZS 3500.3. Clause 5.16 says ballast against flotation is roughly the tank volume in kilograms (for example about 5 tonnes for a 5,000 L tank) unless the maker or an engineer says otherwise.

### Real tank dimensions (Icon-Septech Universal concrete tanks, underground)

Sizes in mm. "Body" is the main wall diameter; "base" is the widest point at the foot or the lid overhang. Heights are overall, outside.

1. **2,100 L (drawing 565V2100U, 2010):** body diameter 1335, base 1385, height 2000. Read from drawing text, not from the picture; confirm on the drawing.
2. **3,800 L (565V3800U, 2010):** diameter 1680, height 2290. Read from drawing text; confirm on the drawing.
3. **5,000 L, Class A (ICON-4TA-GA010, 2009):** body 1860, base 1905, height 2300; 75 mm walls; Class A, 10 kN, maximum fill over the lid 1.0 m; mass 3,615 kg. (The later 565V5000U sheet shows the base as 1915.)
4. **10,500 L, Class A (ICON-4TA-GA005 and 565V10500U):** body 2440, base 2500, height 2700; Class A 10 kN, maximum fill 1.0 m; 6,830 kg.
5. **10,750 L, Class B lid (ICON-4TA-GA004 and 565V10750UB):** base section 3350 diameter, lid 3400 (25 mm overhang all round); base section 1510 high plus 150 lid, about 1660 overall; Class B 80 kN (trafficable), maximum fill 0.6 m; lid 3,215 kg, base 6,385 kg.
6. **20,000 L, Class B lid (ICON-4TA-GA001 and 565V20000UB):** diameter 3400; tank 2600 high plus 150 lid, about 2750 overall; Class B 80 kN, maximum fill 0.6 m; tank 8,935 kg plus lid 3,215 kg.
7. **21,500 L in two sections, Class A (565V21500U):** diameter 3350; two 1510 sections, about 3020 high not counting joints; maximum fill 1.0 m.
8. **32,000 L in three sections, Class A (565V32000U, 2010):** diameter 3350; 1510 + 1350 + 1510, 4370 high not counting joints; maximum fill 1.0 m; sections 5.565 t, 3.545 t and 6.385 t.
9. **Access openings, all models:** standard 530 x 580; rectangular option 580 x 1055.
10. **Under-driveway use (maker's project sheets):** 40,000 L as two trafficable Class B tanks and 60,000 L as three, buried under driveways. That points to 20,000 L Class B units. The maker's "Rainsafe" sheet offers Class D (semi-trailer) rating, risers 600 x 600 to 1200 x 1200, and riser heights of 150, 300, 600 and 900.

### Notes for the drawing tool

1. **Class A (10 kN) tanks are not for driveways; Class B (80 kN) lids are.** Under a driveway, use the 10,750 L or 20,000 L Class B models, with no more than 0.6 m of fill over the lid.
2. **Draw round underground tanks at the widest diameter** (base or lid overhang), for example 3400 for the 20,000 L Class B tank, not the body diameter.

Researched 19 Nov 2026 (Melbourne). Public web only. No job data used.
Sizes in mm. Slimline sizes are length x width x height. Round sizes are diameter x height.

## ANSWER FIRST

1. **Same volume, different makers: plan sizes differ by about 10 to 15 percent in length and 8 to 30 percent in width.** Heights differ most between models (standard, short base, lowline). So a "typical" size will be wrong on some jobs.
2. **"Slimline" is a generic trade word, not one maker's name.** Makers across the market use it to describe narrow tanks. The only IP Australia record I opened (mark 1313018, a retailer's logo mark) mentions "slimline tanks" only as goods wording. I did not search for a stand-alone "Slimline" word mark; confirm before saying so formally.
3. **Source status.** "Opened" means the page text was read. "Snippet" means a search summary only, not checked at source. Several makers (Tankworld, Bushman, Pioneer, Kingspan) blocked reading, so their figures are snippets.

## 1. Slimline poly

1. **Polymaster, slimline tank pages (opened).** 2,000 L: 2370 x 600 x 2010. 3,000 L: 2570 x 790 x 2050. 5,000 L: 3290 x 1020 x 2010. Page says dimensions are approximate, and Queensland and northern NSW tanks come from a different plant with different sizes.
2. **Tank Factory, upright slimline pages (opened).** 2,000 L: 2250 x 625 x 1930. 5,000 L: 3080 x 940 x 2170.
3. **ASC Tanks, "Slimline Water Tanks Melbourne" page, Rotoplus brand (opened).**
   - 1,000 L: 1900 x 390 x 1800.
   - 2,000 L: 2000 x 620 x 2000 (short base), 2400 x 565 x 2000 (super slimline), 2900 x 750 x 1200 (lowline).
   - 2,500 L: 2180 x 680 x 2150.
   - 3,000 L: 2900 x 650 x 2000 (super slimline), 2400 x 780 x 2200 (short base).
   - 4,000 L: 2650 x 810 x 2200.
   - 5,000 L: 2900 x 1000 x 2100.
4. **Bushman, 3,000 L slimline (opened via the ASC product listing).** 2480 x 810 x 2150. Bushman's own page (snippet) says 2.48 m x 0.80 m x 2.14 m.
5. **Tankworld specification sheets (snippet only).** 3,000 L about 3010 x 700 x 2075. 5,000 L about 3180 x 1080 x 2060. A snippet says dimensions "can vary slightly due to shrinkage".
6. **Gaps.** No 3,500 L slimline poly from a named maker. No 4,000 L from Polymaster or Bushman. No 10,000 L or 22,500 L slimline.

## 2. Slimline steel

1. **Tank Factory, slimline steel page (opened; sizes nominal).**
   - 1,000 L: 1300 x 550 x 1560.
   - 2,000 L: 1700 x 700 x 1860, or 2500 x 550 x 1560 (skinny).
   - 2,500 L: 2200 x 800 x 1560.
   - 3,000 L: 2200 x 800 x 1860, 2500 x 700 x 1860, or 2500 x 850 x 1560.
   - 4,000 L: 2400 x 1000 x 1860, or 2900 x 800 x 1860.
   - 5,000 L: 2600 x 1150 x 1860, 3200 x 900 x 1860, or 3100 x 1150 x 1560.
   - No 3,500 L listed. Made to measure on request.
2. **Kingspan steel slim, Bunnings listings.** 2,000 L: "850 x 1560 x 1900" and "700 x 1860 x 1700", dimension order not labelled and inconsistent (opened). The 3,500 L and 4,000 L listings (snippet) look mislabelled; treat as unreliable.
3. **Fielders and Lysaght:** not found.
4. **Steel versus poly at the same volume:** steel is shorter, wider and lower (3,000 L steel 2200 x 800 x 1860 against poly about 2500 x 800 x 2100).

## 3. Round tanks

1. **10,000 L poly.** Polymaster 2500 diameter x 2380 high (opened, "approximate only"). Urban Poly via ASC 2600 x 2100 (opened). PolyChoice 2480 x 2350 and Tankworld 2540 x 2360 (snippets).
2. **10,000 L steel.** Tank Factory 2700 x 1860 (opened, nominal). A Yarrawonga steel listing 2700 x 2120 (snippet).
3. **22,500 L.** ASC listing of Aquaplate round steel: 3100 x 3070 (opened). Tank Factory's nearest is 22,900 L at 3550 x 2320 (opened). Pioneer corrugated sizes not found.
4. **Small round steel, Tank Factory (opened, nominal).** 2,000 L: 1200 x 1860. 3,000 L: 1500 x 1860. 4,000 L: 1700 x 1860. 5,000 L: 1900 x 1860.
5. **1,000, 2,500, 3,500 L round:** not found.

## 4. Pad and base

1. **Tank Factory installation guide (opened).** Level compacted ground with 75 to 100 mm of fine stone dust inside a solid edge, at least 100 mm larger than the tank each way. Concrete alternative: 75 to 100 mm thick, F72 mesh, 25 MPa; ask an engineer.
2. **Urban Poly via ASC (opened).** Concrete slab preferred; compacted 100 mm sand acceptable for round tanks with protected edges.
3. **ASC 22,500 L page (opened).** 100 mm slab with mesh, at least 50 mm larger than the tank on all sides.
4. **Coerco guide (opened).** Concrete 75 to 100 mm with mesh or rebar. No oversize stated.
5. **Snippets only.** Compacted base extending 300 mm past the walls; one maker asking 500 mm larger with a retaining beam; Tankworld 100 mm larger each side. So makers range from 50 to 500 mm oversize.

## 5. Clearances

1. **Tank Factory (opened):** at least 50 mm air gap next to a house or shed.
2. **Urban Poly via ASC (opened):** at least 100 mm from any fixed object such as a building or fence.
3. **ASC 22,500 L (opened):** at least 550 mm clear above the tank for service.
4. **Bushman (opened via ASC):** delivery needs vehicle turning space and clear access; not a tank clearance.
5. **Boundary, eave and building-code setbacks:** no maker publishes them. They come from Victorian planning and building rules, which I did not research.

## RECOMMENDATION

1. **Offer both.** A short list of maker presets, plus length and width typed by the drafter, with the typed values always winning.
2. **When only the volume is known, draw a generic "volume to default size" tank** using the middle of the ranges above, and label it "generic, confirm with maker". For example: slimline poly 2,000 L about 2300 x 600, 3,000 L about 2500 x 800, 5,000 L about 3100 x 1000. Round 10,000 L about 2500 diameter.
3. **Draw a pad outline larger than the tank**, default 100 mm each side, editable, so the drafter can widen it for makers who ask more.
4. **Show the maker and the source beside each preset**, and warn that sizes are approximate and vary by plant and model.
5. **Steel versus poly and round versus slimline should be separate presets**, because their shapes differ for the same volume.

## What I could not confirm

1. Tankworld, Bushman, Pioneer and Kingspan figures, which are snippets only.
2. 3,500 L slimline poly, 4,000 L Polymaster and Bushman, and 1,000, 2,500 and 3,500 L round.
3. Fielders, Lysaght, Rhino, Stormtank, Clark, Elkhorn, Enduro and Aqua Tanks sizes.
4. Numeric tolerance (only wording such as "approximate" and "nominal").
5. A stand-alone "Slimline" word mark on the IP Australia register.
6. Clearances to fences, eaves and boundaries, and Victorian setback rules.
