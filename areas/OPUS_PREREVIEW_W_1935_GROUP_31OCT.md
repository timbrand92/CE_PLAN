### PR #1938, B-122 BUG-1193: curved boundary

1. **What changes.** In CDPROFILE, a boundary polyline side with a bulge, and an ARC on the boundary layer, are followed in straight pieces of 5 degrees or less (`ViewModels/LongitudinalSectionViewModel.cs` 1275-1312, `AddBoundaryCurve` 1361-1373, maths in `Helpers/LongSectionBoundaryProjector.cs` 103-128). The pieces go through the existing crossing code, so the mark lands where the curve really crosses the pipe.
2. **Geometry check.** The centre offset `(chord/2)(1 - b²)/(2b)` to the left of the chord, and the sweep `4·atan(b)`, are the standard bulge formulas. They hold for bulges above 1 too (the centre moves to the right). A polyline side is read in its own plane (`GetPoint2dAt` plus `Elevation`) and turned to the world with `Matrix3d.PlaneToWorld(normal)` before the Xref transform. An ARC is turned into its plane and back with the same normal, so its sweep direction is right. A mirrored survey boundary (normal pointing down) is the one case where a wrong plane would show; it is not tested.
3. **Against the carpark.** The fixture result (10.00 m, 5.00 m with no bulge) matches. But BUG-1193 also says "if a curve cannot be resolved, warn and leave that mark unresolved; never manufacture a straight-boundary mark". An old-style 2D polyline (`case Polyline2d`, 1314) with arc vertices is still read corner to corner. That is a straight mark from a curve, with no warning. A SPLINE or ELLIPSE is skipped silently. The author lists the 2D-polyline gap (REGRESSION_LOG point 5), but no warning line was added.
4. **Simplification (LOW).** The same file already steps pipe arcs at 5 degrees (`FlattenPolyline`, 904-930). That is now two copies of one idea (design rule #1). It could reuse `ArcPoints`. Not a blocker.
5. **Switch.** `survey.longSectionBoundaryFollowsCurves`, ON (`UserSettings.cs` 2874). It is pinned through both start-up reads, and a typed false is kept. OFF reads the bulge as 0 and skips the ARC case (lines 1277 and 1294), which is exactly the old reading. The 5-degree step is a drawing tolerance, not an engineering value; it matches the existing pipe stepping.
6. **R-27, protection, REDO.** It reads Xref geometry only. No write, regen, view or system variable change.
7. **Merge.** It clashes only in REGRESSION_LOG.md. It merges cleanly with #1939, which edits the same two files in other places.
