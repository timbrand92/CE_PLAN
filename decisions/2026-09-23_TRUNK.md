# The plugin's trunk — Tim, 23 September 2026

**Question:** the plugin's `master` and the live line installed in AutoCAD (`10ac480d`, branch `claude/cdparts-save-reset`) had diverged by 80 and 988 commits since 31 Aug 2026. Which is the trunk?

**What master held that live did not (80 commits):** plan and design documents (V3 window and icon design pack, Structural Level 3 plan, drafting production plan, pump station and council evidence records) and the 19 to 20 Sep privacy clean-up (PRs #79, #80, #81). No drawing behaviour.

**What live held that master did not (988 commits, 17 Jul to 15 Sep):** all Civil drawing work tested on the September test days (CDEDIT, CDWT, CDSTRIP, CDCHECK, CDPROFILE, CDRULES and more), the pit preview, CDPARTS Save and Reset, shared code, Structural and Manager changes.

**Tim's answer, 23 Sep 2026 (after asking "explain more first"):** *"Live is the trunk"*.

**What it means:** master's 80 commits are merged into the live line; master then points at that combined line. From here every merge to master reaches the bundle at the next deploy, and register #78's rule "branch from LIVE, not from master" becomes "branch from master". Nothing in AutoCAD changes until the next deploy on Tim's phrase.
