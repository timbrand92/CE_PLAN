   1. **LandXML pit inverts and sump levels** (`LandXmlParser.cs:287`, `:307`): an `<Invert elev="0">` or `elev="-12.5"` used to be dropped and is now kept as IL 0.000 or IL -12.500.    2. **Pit schedule cover levels** (`XcivilScheduleService.cs:752`, through `ExtractImportedScheduleCoverLevel`): every two-decimal number in a schedule row is a candidate, and only "reasonable levels" count. With that rule OFF, the first candidate is taken, so a leading 0.00 becomes the cover. This is a new way to lose a cover level that the PR's tests do not cover.
   3. **Schedule row checks** (`:909`, `:949`) accept rows with a cover of 0 or above 250 m that are rejected today; **derived inverts** (`:589`) likewise.
